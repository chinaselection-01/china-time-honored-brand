Brand Story

Founded in Beijing by Mr. Dai Yuexuan, a master brush maker who inherited the traditional techniques of Huzhou brush-making, Dai Yuexuan has been a hallmark of Chinese calligraphy culture since the late Qing Dynasty and early Republic period. During that time, Beijing thrived as a cultural hub, attracting scholars and artists with a high demand for quality brushes. Mr. Dai, with his exquisite craftsmanship and meticulous selection of materials, produced brushes with sharp, resilient tips that were highly prized by calligraphers and painters. His shop, located on the historic Liulichang Cultural Street, became a gathering place for literati and artists. Dai Yuexuan brushes are not only practical tools but also carriers of profound cultural heritage, witnessing the flourishing of modern Chinese painting and calligraphy. The craft has been passed down through generations, with each brush still handmade through dozens of steps, preserving traditional quality and making Dai Yuexuan an enduring symbol of Beijing's cultural identity.

Cultural Note

Dai Yuexuan's brush-making technique inherits the Huzhou tradition, emphasizing processes such as material selection, water basin shaping, and tying. Each brush undergoes dozens of handcrafted steps. The brush heads are round and straight with sharp tips, allowing smooth writing, showcasing the excellence of Chinese brush craftsmanship. The brand adheres to tradition while integrating Beijing's cultural essence, making it a representative of Beijing's intangible cultural heritage.
