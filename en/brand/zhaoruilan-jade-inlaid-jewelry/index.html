Brand Story

Zhao Ruilan originated in Shanghai, closely tied to the city's commercial prosperity after it opened as a treaty port in the mid-19th century. The founder, whose name is yet to be verified, established a jewelry shop during the Tongzhi era (1862-1875) of the Qing Dynasty. With a keen eye for jade, gold, and silver, and exceptional craftsmanship, the brand quickly earned customers' trust. The name 'Ruilan' symbolizes auspiciousness and elegance, reflecting traditional Chinese aesthetics and business ethics. Through generations, Zhao Ruilan has upheld handmade techniques, blending Chinese and Western methods to create a unique style. Its pieces are not just ornaments but cultural carriers, witnessing Shanghai's urban transformation and the lives of its people. Even during turbulent times, the brand maintained its reputation, becoming a model of integrity in the industry. Today, though not officially certified, Zhao Ruilan's historical depth and craftsmanship remain highly regarded.

Cultural Note

Zhao Ruilan's craftsmanship inherits the Shanghai-style jewelry tradition, emphasizing meticulous handwork and a fusion of Chinese and Western aesthetics. The production process includes design, wax carving, stone setting, and polishing, with a focus on detail and quality. The brand culture embodies integrity and the pursuit of perfection, epitomizing Shanghai's commercial spirit.
