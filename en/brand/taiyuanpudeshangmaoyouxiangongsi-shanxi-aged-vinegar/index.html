Brand Story

Located in historic Taiyuan, Shanxi, Taiyuan Pude Trading Co., Ltd. traces its origins to the Jin merchant firms of the Ming and Qing dynasties (1368-1912). Known for integrity and enterprise, the Jin merchants' legacy is embodied in Pude's name, meaning 'benefiting all under heaven, earning trust across the land,' reflecting the principle of 'righteousness before profit.' Though its founding year is not recorded, the company has long traded in local specialties such as Fenjiu liquor and mature vinegar, serving as a bridge between tradition and modernity. Today, Pude is dedicated to promoting Jin merchant culture and boosting the local economy, continuing this enduring spirit.

Cultural Note

Pude Trading is deeply rooted in Jin merchant culture, emphasizing integrity and balancing righteousness with profit. Its products, such as mature vinegar made through the five-step process of steaming, fermenting,熏,淋, and aging, and Fenjiu's 'double steaming and double fermentation' technique, represent the living heritage of Shanxi's intangible cultural heritage. Through commerce, the company fosters cultural exchange and regional connectivity.
