Brand Story

Airuipai originates from Shandong, with roots tracing back to the Ming and Qing dynasties. According to local records, Shandong has long been a hub of commerce and handicrafts. The brand's predecessor was likely a small workshop renowned for its exquisite skills. During the Qing dynasty, the Grand Canal's prosperity turned Shandong into a distribution center for goods from north and south, allowing Airuipai to expand and sell products far and wide. The name 'Airuipai' means 'loving the people, blessing the world, and passing on craftsmanship,' reflecting Confucian values of benevolence and integrity. In modern times, Airuipai has embraced modern management while preserving core handmade techniques to ensure authentic quality. Its products carry the memories of Shandong's people and are an integral part of China's time-honored brand culture.

Cultural Note

Airuipai's techniques integrate traditional Shandong folk food processing wisdom, adhering to the principles of 'careful selection, strict process, and authentic taste.' Its handmade sauces are naturally sun-brewed, Ejiao糕 follows ancient boiling methods, and pancakes are made using the traditional摊制 technique from central Shandong. These crafts preserve local flavors and embody the profound culinary culture of Qilu.
