Brand Story

Founded in the late Qing Dynasty and early Republic of China, the exact year of Lüliuju's establishment remains unknown. Its name, inspired by the poetic line 'a household nestled deep in green willows,' reflects elegance and tranquility. Initially specializing in vegetarian dishes, it gained popularity among local Buddhist devotees and literati. During the Republic era, Lüliuju grew to become a representative of Nanjing's vegetarian scene. Its culinary techniques emphasize the natural flavors of ingredients, merging tradition with innovation to create a unique style. After the founding of New China, the brand underwent public-private partnership while preserving its traditional craftsmanship and continuously introducing new offerings. Today, Lüliuju stands as a symbol of Jiangsu's vegetarian culture, carrying memories across generations and embodying the profound heritage of Chinese culinary arts.

Cultural Note

Lüliuju's vegetarian techniques adhere to the principle of 'pure vegetarianism' (jingsu), excluding pungent vegetables like onions and garlic, and focusing on ingredient pairing and precise heat control. The craft, passed down through generations, integrates the essence of Jiangnan culinary culture, showcasing the depth and breadth of Chinese vegetarian traditions.
