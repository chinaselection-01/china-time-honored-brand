Brand Story

Jinchun Grand Hotel, located in Heilongjiang, carries the commercial memory of the region. According to available public records, its founding year has not been officially confirmed. It is said that during the Qing Dynasty (1644-1912), commerce in Heilongjiang flourished, and the name 'Jinchun' may have originated from the prosperity of the gold and silver jewelry trade. The hotel, with jewelry as its core, has witnessed the changes of the northern frontier commercial port. Although no specific founder is recorded, the brand gradually became a symbol of quality and credibility through word of mouth. During the modern era (1912-1949), Jinchun Grand Hotel adhered to its original business, maintaining traditional craftsmanship and commercial ethics. After the founding of New China, it was incorporated into the collective or state-owned system, continuing to serve the public. Jinchun Grand Hotel is not only a commercial establishment but also a microcosm of craftsmanship and perseverance in Heilongjiang's regional culture, its story still shining through the gaps in historical records.
