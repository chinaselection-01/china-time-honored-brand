Brand Story

Baoqing Pai originates from Jiangsu, with its founding year yet to be officially verified, but according to local historical records and industry anecdotes, it can be traced back to the mid-to-late Qing Dynasty (around the 1800s). The brand name 'Baoqing' symbolizes 'treasures gathered and blessings born,' conveying wishes for good fortune and wealth. In its early days, Baoqing Pai was famous for handcrafted gold and silver jewelry, particularly excelling in filigree inlay (huasi xiangqian) and kingfisher feather art (diancui), which were favored by ladies in the Jiangnan region. Legend has it that during the Daoguang reign (1821-1850), a craftsman named Shen Wansan (to be verified) opened the 'Baoqing Silver House' in Suzhou, earning a reputation for exquisite skill and honest business. The Shen family carried on the tradition, integrating Suzhou craftsmanship with Shanghai style, making Baoqing Pai a representative of Jiangnan jewelry culture. During the Republic of China era (1912-1949), Baoqing Pai crafted custom jewelry for Shanghai elites, witnessing the social changes of modern China. Today, despite the passage of time, Baoqing Pai remains committed to traditional techniques and the inheritance of Chinese jewelry culture. Its works not only carry historical memories but also incorporate modern aesthetics, serving as a cultural bridge between the past and the future.

Cultural Note

Baoqing Pai's craftsmanship blends the delicacy of Suzhou workmanship with the fashion of Shanghai style, emphasizing handcraft and artisan spirit. It inherits intangible cultural heritage techniques such as filigree and kingfisher feather art, reflecting the essence of Jiangnan jewelry culture. The brand upholds the philosophy of 'objects carrying the Way' (qi yi zai dao), conveying auspiciousness and aesthetic appreciation.
