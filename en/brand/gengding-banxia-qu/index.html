Brand Story

Gengding Pharmacy, originating from Sichuan, derives its name from the motto 'Geng metal stands firm, benefiting the world and saving lives.' Though its exact founding year is not officially documented, local chronicles and oral histories trace its roots back to the mid-Qing Dynasty. During that era, Sichuan experienced a population surge due to the 'Huguang Filling Sichuan' migration, leading to frequent epidemics and a thriving herbal medicine market. The founder, a herb gatherer from western Sichuan whose name awaits verification, was well-versed in the properties of local herbs. He established a pharmacy in Chengdu, earning a reputation for 'selecting premium ingredients, meticulous processing, and effective remedies.' The processing techniques integrated ancient methods such as 'nine steaming and nine sun-drying' and 'combined water-fire processing,' with signature products like Gengding Banxia Qu and Gengding Fupian, highly regarded by regional physicians. Gengding also emphasized medical practice, collaborating with resident doctors to develop prescriptions, promoting the integration of medicine and pharmacy. Through wars and changing times, Gengding upheld its principle of 'integrity first, genuine medicines at fair prices,' witnessing the evolution of Sichuan's herbal commerce and processing craftsmanship, embodying the essence of Bashu traditional Chinese medicine culture.

Signature Products

Gengding Banxia Qu

Made from authentic Pinellia ternata with ginger juice and alum, fermented and steamed-dried, this remedy resolves phlegm and relieves coughs. A representative of Sichuan-style processing. (50-150 RMB/box)

Gengding Fupian

Prepared from Jiangyou aconite roots through soaking in bile salt, steaming, and slicing to reduce toxicity while preserving efficacy. A key herb for warming yang and dispelling cold. (80-200 RMB/jin)

Gengding Medicinal Wine

Infused with Sichuan herbs, this wine dispels wind-dampness, relaxes tendons, and invigorates collaterals. A popular health tonic. (100-300 RMB/bottle)

Cultural Note

Gengding's processing techniques follow the principle of 'respecting tradition without being bound by it,' emphasizing that 'the efficacy of medicine relies on proper processing.' Methods like 'nine steaming and nine sun-drying' and 'combined water-fire processing' inherit the legacy of the 'Lei Gong Pao Zhi Lun' while adapting to Sichuan's humid climate, forming the distinctive 'Chuan' (Sichuan) style. Gengding adheres to the concept of 'medicine and pharmacy share the same origin,' prioritizing authentic herbs and clinical effectiveness. Its business philosophy and techniques are integral to Sichuan's herbal culture.
