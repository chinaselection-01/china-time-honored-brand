Brand Story

Rongxintang, originating from Shanxi, derives its name from the auspicious meaning of 'glory and prosperity'. Although the exact founding year is yet to be officially verified, local historical records and folk heritage suggest its history can be traced back to the Ming and Qing dynasties (1368-1912 AD). During that period, Shanxi merchants thrived and the herbal medicine trade flourished, giving rise to Rongxintang, which became renowned for its honest business practices and exceptional medical skills. The founder, whose name is yet to be confirmed, was well-versed in the principles of herbal medicine and advocated the concept of 'medicine and food share the same origin'. He combined authentic medicinal materials with folk remedies, developing unique processing techniques. Through generations, Rongxintang has adhered to the ancestral motto of 'selecting superior materials and processing with precision'. Its prescriptions and skills have been passed down orally within the family, making it an important epitome of Shanxi's traditional Chinese medicine culture. Culturally, Rongxintang is not just a pharmacy but a crystallization of the Jin merchants' spirit and the wisdom of Chinese medicine, witnessing the evolution of regional medical culture. Its story has been passed down by word of mouth, symbolizing the resilience of time-honored brands.

Cultural Note

Rongxintang upholds the ancestral motto: 'Even in complex processing, never reduce effort; even with precious ingredients, never cut corners.' Its techniques cover cleaning, cutting, and processing, with particular expertise in steaming, boiling, calcining, and stir-frying. The herbs are sourced from the authentic production areas of the Taihang and Lüliang mountains, emphasizing 'authenticity, timeliness, and cleanliness'. Culturally, Rongxintang integrates the integrity of Jin merchants with the TCM concept of 'preventive treatment', focusing on the homology of medicine and food. Its products are not just medicines but also carriers of health-preserving culture.
