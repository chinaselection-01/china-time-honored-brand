Brand Story

Xuehua Beer traces its roots to Shenyang, Liaoning, with a history dating back to the mid-20th century. In 1957, the Shenyang Brewery was formed through the merger of several small workshops under the public-private partnership policy, becoming a key beer producer in Northeast China. In 1964, the brewery successfully developed the 'Xuehua' (Snowflake) brand, named to symbolize 'purity like snow, freshness like flowers.' It quickly gained popularity in the Northeast. After the reform and opening-up, Xuehua Beer became a well-known brand across Liaoning and the whole country, thanks to its consistent quality and affordable price. In 1993, China Resources acquired the Shenyang Brewery, marking the beginning of Xuehua's national expansion. More than just a beverage, Xuehua Beer has witnessed the rise, decline, and transformation of Liaoning's old industrial base, carrying the collective memories of generations. Its brewing process combines tradition and innovation, using high-quality barley and pure water, fermented at low temperatures to achieve a crisp and smooth taste.

Cultural Note

Xuehua Beer's brewing technique inherits traditional beer craftsmanship, integrated with modern temperature-controlled fermentation. It features selected two-row barley and deep groundwater, processed through mashing, fermentation, and filtration to ensure a clear, transparent body with fine, persistent foam. The brand emphasizes quality stability while incorporating the hearty culture of Northeast China, making it a national brand for everyday consumption.
