Brand Story

Longshunrong, a prestigious Chinese medicine brand in Tianjin, traces its origins to the Qianlong period (c. 1736-1795). It is said that the founder, from the Yue family, branched off from Beijing's Tongrentang and established a pharmacy in Tianjin, naming it 'Longshunrong' to symbolize prosperity, smoothness, and the shade of the banyan tree. The pharmacy focused on trading herbs and ready-made medicines, adhering to the principles of 'selecting superior materials and processing with precision,' which quickly earned it a solid reputation. During the Daoguang era (1821-1850), Longshunrong set up its headquarters on Guyi Street, becoming a key distribution center for medicinal materials in the north. Its hallmark was the strict quality control of herbs, sourced directly from production areas, and the adherence to traditional processing methods such as nine-steaming-nine-sunning, wine preparation, and vinegar preparation. Longshunrong even supplied medicines to the imperial court, enhancing its prestige. In the Republican era, it expanded into producing patent medicines like 'Baochi Powder.' Culturally, it embodies the Yue family's philosophy of 'healing and nurturing life,' leaving a lasting impact on the traditional medicine industry in northern China.

Its cultural value lies in preserving the operational model and pharmaceutical standards of northern Chinese medicine shops since the Ming and Qing dynasties, offering insights into Tianjin's commercial history and the development of traditional medicine.
