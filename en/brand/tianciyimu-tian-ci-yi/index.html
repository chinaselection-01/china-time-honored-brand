Brand Story

Tianci Yimu, originating from Henan, carries a profound legacy of Chinese woodcraft. Though its founding year has yet to be officially confirmed, the brand embodies the deep-rooted woodworking traditions of the Central Plains, the cultural heartland of China. Adhering to ancient methods while embracing modern aesthetics, Tianci Yimu is dedicated to crafting wooden masterpieces with cultural depth. The brand's spirit aligns with the time-honored craftsmanship of Chinese heritage brands. Its artisans use wood as a medium to convey reverence for nature and dedication to their craft, with each piece reflecting respect for and innovation within traditional culture. While the brand's story is still evolving, its commitment to woodcraft has already become a shining thread in the tapestry of Chinese wood culture.
