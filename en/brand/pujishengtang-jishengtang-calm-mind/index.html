Brand Story

Pu Jishengtang traces its origins to Zhejiang, where the Pu family practiced medicine as early as the Ming Dynasty (1368-1644). According to local chronicles and genealogical records, they were renowned for their 'benevolent heart and skilled hands.' During the Qianlong reign (1736-1795), descendant Pu Wenyuan established a formal practice in Hangzhou, hanging the plaque 'Pu Jishengtang' and specializing in epidemic diseases and internal ailments. His self-made herbal pills and ointments gained fame for their efficacy. In the Guangxu era (1875-1908), Pu Jing'an compiled the 'Jishengtang Prescription Collection' and set the motto 'Healing as the foundation, integrity as the principle.' Despite wars and upheavals, the brand has preserved ancient processing methods, including the 'nine-steam-nine-sun' technique and the selection of authentic medicinal herbs, embodying the precision and pragmatism of Jiangnan medicine. Today, Pu Jishengtang stands as a cultural symbol of Chinese time-honored brands, carrying the medical ethics and craftsmanship of the Pu family through generations.

Cultural Note

Pu Jishengtang adheres to the precept: 'No matter how complex the processing, never skimp on effort; no matter how costly the ingredients, never reduce the quality.' It uses 'Zhe Ba Wei' (eight famous Zhejiang herbs) and other authentic regional materials, with techniques emphasizing soaking, steaming, drying, and stir-frying, especially the 'nine-steam-nine-sun' method to harmonize medicinal properties. The motto 'Healing as the foundation, integrity as the principle' reflects the fusion of Confucian medical ethics and commerce, a typical example of 'medicine-merchant unity' in northern Zhejiang's TCM culture.
