Brand Story

Founded in the late Qing Dynasty, Beijing Jiqingtang traces its origins to Wang Jiqing, a former imperial physician who left the court to establish the pharmacy, wishing for auspiciousness and well-being. Renowned for its carefully selected authentic herbs and traditional processing methods, especially its pills, powders, ointments, and pellets, Jiqingtang earned the trust of Beijing residents. Over a century, it has upheld the motto 'Integrity as the foundation, benevolence to heal the world,' integrating traditional medicine with modern technology to innovate continuously. Its cultural significance lies not only in preserving the essence of Chinese medicine but also in reflecting the commitment of a time-honored brand to quality and responsibility.

Cultural Note

Jiqingtang adheres to the ancient principle: 'Even if processing is complex, never reduce effort; even if ingredients are precious, never cut costs.' Combining traditional craftsmanship with modern technology, it ensures product quality. Its culture emphasizes 'benevolent healing' and medical ethics, reflecting the core values of traditional Chinese medicine.
