Brand Story

Zaocun, originating from Beijing, is steeped in the rich heritage of Chinese clothing culture. Its roots can be traced back to the Ming (1368-1644) and Qing (1644-1912) dynasties, when Beijing, as the imperial capital, attracted skilled artisans from across the country, particularly in the textile and apparel industry. The name 'Zaocun' means 'creating the perfect measure,' reflecting an unwavering pursuit of precision in tailoring and craftsmanship. While the exact founding year and key figures are yet to be officially confirmed, historical records indicate that Beijing housed official dyeing and weaving bureaus during the Ming dynasty and became a major production center for court attire in the Qing dynasty. Zaocun likely draws from this tradition, blending imperial and folk techniques to develop a distinctive style. Its cultural significance lies in preserving the essence of Beijing's clothing culture and showcasing the refinement and elegance of traditional Chinese craftsmanship.

Cultural Note

Zaocun's techniques integrate traditional crafts such as Beijing embroidery (Jingxiu), frog buttons (pankou), and piping and edging (xianggun). The brand emphasizes meticulous fabric selection and precise cutting, embodying the artisan spirit of 'measuring the body to tailor the garment.' Its cultural value lies in preserving Beijing's regional clothing characteristics and offering insights into the evolution of Ming and Qing dynasty attire.
