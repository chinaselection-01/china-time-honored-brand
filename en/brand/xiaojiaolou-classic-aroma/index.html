Brand Story

Xiaojiaolou liquor originates from Sichuan, with a history tracing back to the Ming and Qing dynasties. During the late Ming and early Qing, wars devastated Sichuan's population, and the subsequent 'Huguang Filling Sichuan' migration brought advanced brewing techniques from Hubei and Hunan. In northeastern Sichuan, locals began distilling spirits using quality sorghum and wheat, leveraging the region's unique natural conditions. The name 'Xiaojiaolou' derives from the distillery's location—nestled by mountains and rivers, resembling a corner tower (jiaolou). By the late Qing and early Republic era, the workshop had gained renown for its aromatic, sweet, and crisp liquor, a favorite at local banquets. After 1949, the Xiaojiaolou Distillery was established on the foundation of the traditional workshop, preserving ancient methods while refining techniques. Today, Xiaojiaolou stands as a representative of Sichuan baijiu, carrying the culinary culture and historical memory of northeastern Sichuan.

Cultural Note

Xiaojiaolou's brewing technique inherits the Sichuan-style xiaoqu solid-state fermentation process, emphasizing pit maintenance and microbial environment. Traditional methods like 'continued grain feeding' and 'mixed steaming and boiling' showcase the unique brewing wisdom of northeastern Sichuan. Its liquor culture blends immigrant heritage with local customs, serving as a vital carrier of regional culinary identity.
