Brand Story

Shengshengtang, originating from Shandong, derives its name from the philosophy of 'endless vitality and healing the world.' According to local chronicles and family records, during the Qianlong reign of the Qing Dynasty (1736-1795), the Wang family, a renowned lineage of traditional Chinese medicine practitioners in Jinan, Shandong, was celebrated for their exceptional skills and benevolent care. Around 1775, Wang Dechang (c. 1740-1810), a descendant of the Wang family, established a pharmacy in Jinan, naming it Shengshengtang. Wang Dechang, well-versed in herbal medicine and meticulous in processing, produced pills, powders, ointments, and elixirs of remarkable efficacy, earning the deep trust of the people. Adhering to the motto 'select premium ingredients, refine with precision, and serve all with honesty,' Shengshengtang flourished through generations, becoming a reputable pharmacy in Shandong by the late Qing and early Republic era (c. 1900). Its cultural significance lies in embodying the profound heritage of Qilu medicine and the Confucian principle of 'benevolence' in practice, with its philosophy of 'integrity in business and righteousness in profit' profoundly influencing the development of traditional Chinese medicine.

Cultural Note

Shengshengtang preserves ancient processing techniques, emphasizing the authenticity of herbs and precise control of heat during preparation. Processes like 'nine-steam and nine-sun-dry' (jiu zheng jiu shai) ensure the potency of its products. In business, it adheres to 'integrity first, quality foremost,' reflecting the Confucian merchant spirit of the Qilu business community.
