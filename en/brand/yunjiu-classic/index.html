Brand Story

Yunjiu is produced in Duyun City, Qiannan Buyi and Miao Autonomous Prefecture, Guizhou Province, with a brewing history dating back to the Ming and Qing dynasties. Historical records indicate that as early as the Hongwu period (1368-1398 AD), local ethnic minorities were already fermenting spirits using glutinous rice and sorghum in natural limestone caves, establishing a distinctive brewing tradition. By the late Qing and early Republic era (1900-1949), numerous distilleries flourished in Duyun, with the renowned Zhang Family Distillery leading the way. Their 'Yunxiang' (Yun-aroma) technique, which integrated the wisdom of local ethnic groups, gradually defined Yunjiu's signature flavor. In 1950, the state-owned Duyun Distillery was established by consolidating several workshops, officially adopting the brand name 'Yunjiu'. Beyond its culinary significance, Yunjiu bears witness to the cultural exchange and integration of local communities. Its brewing technique has been inscribed on the Guizhou Provincial Intangible Cultural Heritage list, making it a vital component of the province's baijiu heritage.

Cultural Note

Yunjiu's brewing technique embodies the collective wisdom of local ethnic minorities, utilizing natural cave cellaring to cultivate a unique microbial environment. The process strictly adheres to traditional procedures—including starter making, fermentation, distillation, and aging—with an emphasis on locally sourced, eco-friendly ingredients. Yunjiu culture reflects the historical heritage and ethnic distinctiveness of the Qiannan region, making it a significant representative of Guizhou's liquor culture.
