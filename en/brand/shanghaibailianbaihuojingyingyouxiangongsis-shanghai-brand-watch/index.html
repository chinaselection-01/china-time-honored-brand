Brand Story

Shanghai No.1 Department Store, commonly known as Shiyi Yidian, is located at 830 East Nanjing Road. Its predecessor was the Sun Company (Daxin Gongsi), founded in 1936 by Cai Chang, an overseas Chinese from Australia. As one of Shanghai's four major department stores at the time, it was renowned for pioneering the underground shopping mall and the first escalator in China. In 1953, it was transformed into the state-owned Shanghai No.1 Department Store, becoming the first state-owned retail department store in New China. Since then, it has served as a symbol of Shanghai commerce, hosting countless domestic and international customers. Its customer-first service philosophy and wide range of products have profoundly influenced the development of China's department store industry. The building itself is a protected modern heritage structure, blending Art Deco style, and stands as an iconic landmark on the Nanjing Road Pedestrian Street. More than a shopping destination, it is a vital carrier of Shanghai's urban memory and commercial culture.

Cultural Note

Shiyi Yidian inherits the Shanghai-style commercial culture, emphasizing service details and the 'Three Olds' integrity principle. Its building, a protected modern heritage site, showcases a blend of Chinese and Western Art Deco styles. The store regularly hosts cultural exhibitions to promote domestic brands, serving as a key window into Shanghai's commercial civilization.
