Brand Story

Zhongya traces its origins to Shandong, a province renowned for its profound heritage in traditional Chinese medicine, home to legendary physicians like Bian Que and Cang Gong. While the exact founding year remains unverified, the brand's development is closely tied to the region's time-honored pharmaceutical industry. Adhering to the principles of 'integrity first, quality foremost,' Zhongya integrates modern technology with traditional wisdom to serve public health. The name 'Zhongya' signifies balance, harmony, and a commitment to societal well-being, reflecting the responsibility of medical practitioners. Over the centuries, Zhongya has witnessed the evolution of Shandong's pharmaceutical sector, from traditional apothecaries to modern enterprises, always upholding quality and compassion. Its cultural significance lies in preserving the essence of Qilu medicine and advancing health causes, making it a notable brand among China's time-honored enterprises.
