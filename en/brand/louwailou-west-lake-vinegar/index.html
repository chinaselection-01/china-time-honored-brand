Brand Story

Founded in 1848 by Hong Ruitang at the foot of Gushan Hill by West Lake, Louwailou takes its name from a line by Southern Song poet Lin Sheng: 'Beyond the hills, beyond the green hills, beyond the towers, a tower.' What began as a modest lakeside eatery grew into a celebrated restaurant. During the Republic era, it became a gathering place for literati such as Yu Dafu and Lu Xun, who left behind many anecdotes. After public-private partnership in the 1950s, Louwailou became a symbol of Hangzhou's culinary culture. Its dishes, blending West Lake scenery with Jiangnan techniques, especially vinegar fish and water shield soup, earned it the title 'First Tower of West Lake.' Through wars and changes, Louwailou has upheld tradition, becoming a representative brand of Hangzhou cuisine and an integral part of West Lake's cultural landscape.

“A tower named for its cuisine, a gathering place for literary minds.”

Cultural Note

Louwailou's cooking emphasizes the natural flavors of ingredients, with meticulous attention to heat and seasoning balance, carrying on the Hangzhou style of 'light, fresh, and tender.' Its dishes harmonize with the scenic beauty of West Lake, creating a unique culture of 'dining with a view.' Traditional techniques, such as the precise heat control for vinegar fish and the slow-braising method for Dongpo Pork, reflect the delicacy and elegance of Jiangnan gastronomy.
