Brand Story

Jinlong Jewelry, a well-known brand in Shanghai's jewelry industry, traces its origins back to the early 20th century in the Shanghai Bund era. At that time, as the financial hub of the Far East, Shanghai witnessed a booming jewelry trade, and Jinlong emerged, quickly gaining prominence through its dedication to traditional craftsmanship and innovative design. The founder, whose name is yet to be confirmed, upheld the philosophy 'Gold as pure as gold, integrity as true as a dragon,' infusing traditional Chinese culture into jewelry design and creating numerous classics. Over a century, Jinlong has witnessed Shanghai's transformation from a treaty port to an international metropolis. Its products are cherished by local consumers and exported worldwide. Throughout its development, Jinlong has emphasized the inheritance and innovation of skills, blending the refinement and inclusiveness of Shanghai-style culture into every piece, making it a shining pearl in the heritage of Chinese time-honored brands.

Cultural Note

Jinlong's craftsmanship originates from Shanghai's traditional gold and silver fine work, integrating the essence of Shanghai-style culture with a focus on detail and innovation. Techniques include hand engraving and filigree inlay, with each piece reflecting the spirit of craftsmanship and Chinese aesthetics. The brand insists on using high-quality materials and strict quality control, striving to merge traditional skills with modern aesthetics.
