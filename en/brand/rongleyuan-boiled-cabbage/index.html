Brand Story

Founded in Chengdu around 1911 by Manchu chef Guan Zhengxing, Rongleyuan rose to prominence under the guidance of master chef Lan Guangjian. Lan skillfully blended culinary traditions from northern and southern China, creating classics like 'Boiled Cabbage in Supreme Stock' and nurturing a generation of Sichuan culinary masters. The restaurant's meticulous techniques, precise heat control, and refined seasoning profoundly influenced the development of Sichuan cuisine. Culturally, Rongleyuan symbolizes the elevation of Sichuan dishes from street fare to refined dining, embodying the essence of Chengdu's gastronomic heritage.

Cultural Note

At the heart of Rongleyuan's craft lies the principle of 'one dish, one style; a hundred dishes, a hundred flavors.' The restaurant excels in slow-simmered broths and precise heat management, particularly in clear and milky soups. Its preservation of the '24 flavor profiles of Sichuan cuisine' has greatly impacted the standardization and internationalization of Sichuan cooking. Rongleyuan is also renowned for nurturing talent, earning the nickname 'the Whampoa Academy of Sichuan cuisine.'
