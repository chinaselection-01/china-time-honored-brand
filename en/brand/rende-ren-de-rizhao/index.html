Brand Story

Rende, a tea brand originating from Shandong, traces its roots to the ancient tea-drinking traditions of the region, which date back to the Tang Dynasty. By the Ming and Qing dynasties, tea houses flourished in cities like Jinan and Qingdao. The name 'Rende' is derived from Confucian ideals of 'ren' (benevolence) and 'de' (virtue), reflecting the profound cultural heritage of the Qilu land. Although the founder's identity is not officially recorded, it is said that the family devoted themselves to tea for generations, building a reputation on integrity and quality. Rende tea house initially offered local green teas and famous teas from across the country, emphasizing craftsmanship and earning the trust of local customers. Through wars and changing times, the brand has endured, preserving traditional tea-making techniques and promoting Qilu tea culture. Today, Rende stands as a representative of Shandong tea, dedicated to upholding the spirit of China's time-honored brands and sharing the unique flavor of Shandong tea with the world.

Cultural Note

Rende's tea culture integrates Confucian philosophy from the Qilu region, emphasizing benevolence and virtue in business. The tea-making process follows traditional steps such as pan-firing, rolling, and drying, with careful attention to heat and hand feel. Shandong's tea-growing areas, located at 36 degrees north latitude, experience significant temperature differences between day and night, enriching the tea leaves and creating a distinctive northern tea flavor. Rende embodies this regional character and promotes Shandong tea culture.
