Brand Story

Founded in 1954, China International Travel Service (CITS), formally known as China International Travel Service Head Office Co., Ltd., was established in Beijing with the approval of the State Council during the early years of the People's Republic of China. Initially under the foreign affairs department, it primarily served foreign guests, marking the beginning of modern tourism in China. Under the leadership of its first general manager, Yang Gongsu, and other pioneers, CITS built a nationwide service network and cultivated a large number of professionals, laying the foundation for China's tourism industry. After the reform and opening-up, CITS transitioned to serve the public, introducing inbound, domestic, and outbound travel services, and became an industry benchmark. Its brand culture blends national mission with market spirit, witnessing China's journey from 'foreign affairs reception' to a 'tourism powerhouse'. As a unique service-oriented time-honored brand, CITS embodies the evolution of China's tourism sector.

Cultural Note

As the first travel agency in the People's Republic of China, CITS pioneered modern travel service standards, with its reception procedures and tour guide norms becoming industry models. The brand carries the historical mission of 'people-to-people diplomacy', playing a unique role in promoting international cultural exchange and reflecting the Chinese tradition of inclusiveness and hospitality.
