Brand Story

Dongshan Tea Factory is situated in Dongshan Town, Wuzhong District, Suzhou, Jiangsu, on the shores of Lake Taihu, one of the original production areas of Biluochun tea. Its history can be traced back to the Tang Dynasty, as recorded in Lu Yu's 'The Classic of Tea'. By the Song Dynasty, Dongshan tea had gained widespread fame. During the Ming and Qing dynasties, it was presented as tribute to the imperial court, enhancing its reputation. In modern times, the factory has integrated traditional methods with contemporary techniques, becoming a representative of the local tea industry. Key figures include local tea-growing families who have passed down the art of tea roasting for generations, particularly skilled in hand-rolling and slow baking over gentle heat. Culturally, Dongshan tea embodies the agricultural wisdom and scholarly elegance of the Jiangnan water towns, serving as a significant symbol of Wu culture.

Cultural Note

Dongshan Tea Factory adheres to the traditional Biluochun production techniques, including plucking, sorting, fixing, rolling, shaping into spiral form, and drying, with hand-firing as a distinctive feature. The craft emphasizes the coordination of heat and hand movements, reflecting the refinement and elegance of Jiangnan tea culture.
