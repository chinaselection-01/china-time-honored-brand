Brand Story

Silian Beauty Salon, officially Beijing Silian Beauty & Hairdressing Co., Ltd., traces its roots to 1956, when, in response to the nation's call to support the capital's construction, nearly a hundred technicians and their equipment from Shanghai's four most prestigious barbershops—Huaxin, Violet, Yunchang, and Xiangming—relocated to Beijing. They established the 'Silian Barbershop' at No. 33 Jinyu Hutong, Wangfujing Street. This move brought Shanghai's cutting-edge hairstyling and perming techniques to Beijing, ushering in a new era for the capital's barbering trade. Renowned for its skillful craftsmanship and attentive service, Silian quickly became a trendsetter in Beijing fashion. Even Premier Zhou Enlai once visited for a haircut, further cementing its fame. Over half a century, through historical transformations, Silian has upheld its artisanal spirit, blending Shanghai-style techniques with Beijing's cultural character, earning its place as a living heritage of China's hairdressing industry.

Cultural Note

Silian's craftsmanship embodies the essence of 'precision, finesse, elegance, and charm,' merging the delicacy of Shanghai styling with the grandeur of Beijing aesthetics. Its traditional techniques—perming, updo styling, and shaving—are tailored to each individual, emphasizing line and layering, while preserving the etiquette of old Shanghai barbershops. Silian also maintains a master-apprentice system, ensuring the continuity of its skills, making it a vital sample for studying modern Chinese social and commercial history.
