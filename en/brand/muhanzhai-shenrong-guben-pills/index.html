Brand Story

Founded during the Qing Dynasty, the exact year of Muhan Zhai's establishment remains unrecorded. The name 'Muhan' is said to derive from the story of Han Kang, a legendary physician known for his honest dealings, reflecting the pharmacy's commitment to integrity and healing. The founder, a local Confucian scholar-physician, established the shop in either Hangzhou or Ningbo. Adhering to the motto 'Genuine goods at fair prices, honest to all', Muhan Zhai meticulously selected herbs and followed ancient processing methods, gaining particular fame for its pills, powders, ointments, and pellets. During the late Qing and early Republic periods, it stood alongside renowned pharmacies like Hu Qing Yu Tang. Though specific details of its history have faded over time, its legacy endures in local memory.

Cultural Note

Muhan Zhai adhered to the principle 'Though preparation is unseen, heaven knows the heart', emphasizing traditional processing methods such as nine-steaming-nine-sunning and slow simmering to ensure the purity of medicinal properties. Its business philosophy reflects the integrity of Zhejiang merchants and offers valuable insight into the history of Jiangnan's pharmaceutical industry during the Qing Dynasty.
