Brand Story

Zhongyi's origins lie in Guangdong, where the humid, miasmic climate of Lingnan fostered a unique medical tradition. During the mid-Qing Dynasty, Guangzhou's status as a trading port spurred a thriving TCM industry, and Zhongyi emerged from one of these historic apothecaries, dealing in authentic herbs and traditional remedies. In the early 20th century, as Western medicine gained influence, Zhongyi upheld ancient processing methods while integrating modern pharmaceutical techniques, evolving into a comprehensive enterprise. The founder, [to be verified], championed the philosophy of 'Zhongzheng Renhe, Yiyi Guanzhi' (Uprightness, Benevolence, and Consistency), emphasizing medical ethics and unwavering quality. Zhongyi stands as a living testament to Lingnan TCM, exemplifying the fusion of tradition and technology and offering a Guangdong model for TCM modernization.

Cultural Note

Zhongyi adheres to Lingnan herbal processing techniques such as 'nine-steam-nine-dry' and 'aging', prioritizing authentic medicinal materials. Its manufacturing integrates traditional experience with modern quality control, following the 'Jun-Chen-Zuo-Shi' (monarch-minister-adjuvant-guide) compounding principle. The brand embodies the Lingnan medical ethic of 'physician's benevolence' and the innovative spirit of 'respecting tradition without being bound by it'.
