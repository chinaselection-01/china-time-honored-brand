Brand Story

Damingfu originates from Daming County, Handan, Hebei, historically known as Daming Prefecture, a city with a glorious past. Although the exact founding year of the brand is not officially documented, its food-making techniques have been passed down through generations, deeply rooted in the region's rich culinary traditions. Historical records show that during the Song Dynasty (960-1279), Daming was a key northern stronghold with thriving commerce and a vibrant food culture. In the Ming and Qing dynasties (1368-1912), it became the political, economic, and cultural center of southern Zhili (now Hebei), attracting merchants and gourmets from all over. The brand upholds the philosophy of preserving ancient methods, selecting premium ingredients, and crafting with dedication, merging traditional flavors with contemporary needs. Though its exact inception remains unrecorded, the name Damingfu carries the memory of a millennium-old capital, and every product embodies respect for history and a commitment to quality.
