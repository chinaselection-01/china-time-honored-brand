Brand Story

Bingxin, a jewelry brand from Zhejiang, has its roots in the region's long-standing tradition of fine gold and silver craftsmanship. Although the exact founding year is not officially confirmed, local chronicles and commercial records suggest that Zhejiang was renowned for its exquisite metalwork since ancient times. Bingxin is likely one of the many jewelry workshops that flourished in the late Qing and early Republic era (around 1900). The name 'Bingxin' means 'radiant new light,' symbolizing the inheritance and innovation of craftsmanship. In the absence of definitive historical records, the founder and early development details remain to be verified. The jewelry industry in Zhejiang, deeply influenced by Wuyue culture, emphasizes delicate craftsmanship and elegant design. Bingxin's works often incorporate traditional patterns and Jiangnan aesthetics, serving as tangible carriers of regional culture. Despite its ambiguous history, the brand's existence reflects the prosperity of Zhejiang's jewelry trade and the spirit of 'ingenuity and generational inheritance' inherent in China's time-honored brands.

Cultural Note

Bingxin inherits the traditional gold and silver craftsmanship of Zhejiang, including techniques such as chasing (zanke), filigree (huasi), and inlaying (xiangqian), with a focus on meticulous handwork. Its designs blend the elegance and refinement of Wuyue culture, with motifs drawn from nature and auspicious symbols, embodying the Eastern aesthetic of 'vessels conveying the Way' (qi yi zai dao).
