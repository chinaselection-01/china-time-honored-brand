Brand Story

Zhen Bu Tong Restaurant, located in Luoyang, Henan, traces its origins to the Tang Dynasty. Historical records show that during Empress Wu Zetian's reign, Luoyang Shuixi (water banquet) had already taken shape as a key part of imperial banquets. In the late Qing and early Republic periods, a renowned Luoyang chef refined the traditional water banquet and founded Zhen Bu Tong. The founder, whose name awaits verification, merged imperial court cuisine with local home-style cooking, creating a distinctive flavor. In 1956, the restaurant underwent public-private partnership and became a state-owned enterprise. Despite multiple reforms over the years, it has stayed true to its traditional techniques. Its Shuixi cooking craft has been inscribed on the national intangible cultural heritage list, symbolizing Luoyang's culinary heritage. Zhen Bu Tong is not just a restaurant; it is a living fossil of Luoyang's history and culture, carrying the gastronomic memories of this ancient capital.

Cultural Note

The Shuixi cooking technique at Zhen Bu Tong emphasizes 'soup and water' (tang tang shui shui), focusing on heat control and seasoning, blending the refinement of imperial cuisine with the simplicity of folk dishes. Its methods—braising, stewing, and steaming—preserve the natural flavors of ingredients. Shuixi is not just a meal; it reflects Luoyang people's hospitality and their respect for etiquette.
