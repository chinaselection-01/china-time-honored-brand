Brand Story

Beijing Gongmei Group traces its roots to the Beijing Handicraft Cooperative established in 1954. After several reorganizations, it was formally founded as Beijing Gongmei Group Corporation in 1980. As a flagship of the capital's arts and crafts industry, the group brings together numerous national intangible cultural heritage techniques, including cloisonné (jingtailan), jade carving, ivory carving, carved lacquerware, and filigree inlay. Its products have been presented as state gifts to foreign heads of state, earning a reputation as a golden calling card of Chinese culture. The group not only preserves these endangered traditional crafts but also revitalizes them through innovative design, driving the industrialization and internationalization of the arts and crafts sector.

Signature Products

Cloisonné Art Pieces

Carved Lacquer Screens

Cultural Note

Beijing Gongmei Group is a vital base for the inheritance of traditional crafts such as the 'Eight Wonders of Beijing' (Yanjing Bajue). Its techniques blend imperial aesthetics with folk wisdom, embodying the spirit of Chinese craftsmanship and aesthetic philosophy. Through productive preservation, the group ensures the living transmission of intangible cultural heritage, integrating it with modern design to give new life to traditional arts.
