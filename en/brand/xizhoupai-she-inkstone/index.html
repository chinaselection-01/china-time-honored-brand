Brand Story

Rooted in Huizhou (now Huangshan City), Anhui Province, Shezhou Pai derives its name from the ancient appellation 'Shezhou'. The region has long been celebrated for its scholarly culture and masterful craftsmanship, particularly in the production of writing brushes, ink sticks, paper, inkstones, lacquerware, and bamboo weaving. While the brand's exact founding date is yet to be officially confirmed, its spirit is deeply intertwined with Huizhou's thousand-year-old craft traditions. Historical records indicate that Huizhou crafts flourished during the Tang and Song dynasties, reaching their zenith in the Ming and Qing periods, producing numerous skilled artisans. Shezhou Pai is dedicated to preserving this invaluable heritage by combining traditional techniques with contemporary design, creating exquisite pieces that reflect Huizhou's distinctive character. Although no specific founder is documented, each product embodies the wisdom and dedication of Huizhou artisans, exemplifying the Huizhou merchant spirit of 'striving for excellence and upholding integrity'. More than mere commodities, Shezhou Pai creations serve as vessels of Huizhou culture, allowing people to experience the warmth of history and the richness of cultural heritage in their daily lives.

Cultural Note

Shezhou Pai's craftsmanship is rooted in Huizhou culture, encompassing traditional arts such as inkstone carving, ink stick making, and lacquerware. These crafts demand meticulous material selection and intricate processes—for instance, She inkstone carving involves dozens of steps, while Huizhou ink stick production requires 'a thousand hammerings and hundred refinements'. Skills are traditionally passed down through master-apprentice relationships, reflecting Huizhou's relentless pursuit of quality. Shezhou Pai preserves ancient methods while integrating modern design, revitalizing traditional crafts for contemporary audiences.
