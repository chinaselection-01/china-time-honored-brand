Brand Story

Shuangyu's roots trace back to Jiangsu, with a history that can be traced to the Qing Dynasty, though the exact founding year remains unverified. The brand began with the production of dried meat (roupu), selecting premium pork hind legs, marinating them with a secret sauce, and then undergoing slicing, curing, and roasting. The resulting product, with its glossy red hue and sweet-savory taste, quickly won local acclaim. During the Republic of China era, Shuangyu dried meat had become a renowned specialty of Jiangsu, even reaching markets in Shanghai. After the founding of the People's Republic of China, the Shuangyu Food Factory was established, preserving traditional techniques and gradually expanding production. The name 'Shuangyu' (double fish) draws from the auspicious Chinese motif symbolizing abundance and prosperity, reflecting the cultural aspiration for a better life. As a time-honored brand of Jiangsu, Shuangyu has witnessed the development of the Jiangnan food industry, and its traditional craftsmanship has been listed as an intangible cultural heritage, making it a vital carrier of local culture.

Cultural Note

Shuangyu's production techniques adhere to Jiangnan traditions, emphasizing ingredient selection and precise heat control. The dried meat undergoes over a dozen processes, with the roasting stage being particularly crucial, using charcoal slow-roasting to achieve a tender, flaky texture. The brand name, derived from the auspicious double-fish motif, exemplifies the fusion of cuisine and culture. Its craftsmanship has been recognized as a municipal-level intangible cultural heritage, representing an important facet of Jiangsu's culinary culture.
