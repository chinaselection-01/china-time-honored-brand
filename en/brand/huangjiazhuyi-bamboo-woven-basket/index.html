Brand Story

Huang's Bamboo Art originates from Zhejiang, with a history tracing back to the Ming and Qing dynasties (1368-1912 AD). According to legend, the Huang ancestors moved to Zhejiang during the Qianlong reign (1736-1795 AD) and made a living by bamboo weaving, renowned for their superb skills. The Huang family has been dedicated to bamboo craftsmanship for generations, preserving ancient techniques while incorporating the elegance of Jiangnan, gradually forming a unique style. By the Republic of China era (1912-1949), Huang's Bamboo Art had grown considerably, with products sold to surrounding regions. After the founding of New China, the craft was preserved and developed, producing works that are both practical and artistic, beloved by the public. Huang's Bamboo Art is not just a craft but a microcosm of Jiangnan culture, reflecting the wisdom of harmonious coexistence between humans and nature. However, the exact founding year and corporate affiliation are yet to be officially verified.

Cultural Note

Huang's Bamboo Art employs traditional handcrafting techniques, including splitting bamboo strips, weaving, and edge finishing, using high-quality local moso bamboo. The craft emphasizes the interweaving of warp and weft, resulting in fine, dense textures that are both durable and flexible. Culturally, bamboo weaving embodies the agricultural civilization and folk aesthetics of Jiangnan, reflecting virtues of frugality and resilience.
