Brand Story

Leishi traces its origins to 1662, during the Qing Dynasty, when the renowned physician Lei Yunshang founded Songfentang pharmacy in Suzhou, later relocating to Shanghai. Lei, a celebrated doctor of the Wu region, was famed for his exceptional medical skills and effective remedies, particularly the Liushen Wan (Six Spirit Pills). The Lei family upheld the motto of 'selecting authentic ingredients and refining with precision,' blending traditional Chinese pharmaceutical techniques with Jiangnan culture. Over centuries, Leishi has preserved ancient formulas while integrating modern medical practices, showcasing the living heritage of TCM and its enduring commercial ethics.

Signature Products

Liushen Wan (Six Spirit Pills)

A classic Leishi formula known for clearing heat, detoxifying, reducing swelling, and relieving pain. It is a renowned remedy for throat ailments. (Approx. 50-100 RMB per box)

Leishi Renshen Zaizao Wan

Used for stroke-induced hemiplegia, this pill dispels wind, resolves phlegm, and promotes blood circulation. (Approx. 30-80 RMB per box)

Leishi Xingjun San

A traditional Chinese patent medicine for summer-heat dampness causing vomiting and diarrhea, with effects of dispelling filth and opening orifices. (Approx. 20-50 RMB per bottle)

Cultural Note

Leishi's craftsmanship centers on the 'Leiyunshang Songfentang Traditional Chinese Medicine Preparation Methods,' including the processing of pills, powders, ointments, and pellets. The micro-pill making technique of Liushen Wan exemplifies the TCM principle of 'small pills, powerful effect.' The brand merges the Wu School of Medicine with Shanghai's commercial spirit, emphasizing authentic sourcing and standardized processing, thereby transmitting the rich pharmaceutical culture of Jiangnan.
