Brand Story

Tongjitang was established around 1888 during the Qing Dynasty by Luo Zhi'an, a renowned physician from Guizhou. Luo, deeply versed in herbal medicine, personally traveled across Yunnan, Guizhou, and Sichuan to source authentic medicinal materials. With the mission of 'Tongji all beings' (benefiting all), he strictly followed ancient processing methods. His prescriptions, rooted in classical texts, were especially famous for their ointments, pills, powders, and pellets, serving the local community. During the Republican era, Luo's descendants continued the business, adhering to 'genuine goods at fair prices, cheating neither young nor old,' maintaining quality even amid wartime. After 1949, Tongjitang was integrated into the state system, preserving its traditional techniques. In 2006, it was recognized as a China Time-honored Brand, and its pharmaceutical craftsmanship was listed as a provincial intangible cultural heritage. Tongjitang stands as a testament to the development of traditional Chinese medicine in Guizhou and a vital representative of the region's medical culture.

Cultural Note

Tongjitang adheres to the tradition of 'ancient processing methods,' using authentic herbs from Yunnan, Guizhou, and Sichuan. Its techniques include stir-frying, roasting, calcining, and steaming, with the 'nine-steam nine-sun' process being particularly renowned. The pharmacy's philosophy emphasizes 'medicine for healing, and healing through precise medicine,' highlighting the quality and efficacy of herbs, reflecting the TCM principle of 'harmony between heaven and humanity.'
