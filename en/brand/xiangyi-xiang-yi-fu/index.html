Brand Story

Xiangyi traces its roots to Yiyang, Hunan, located in the golden tea-growing belt at 28°N latitude. Yiyang has been a tea-producing region since ancient times, with Lu Yu's 'The Classic of Tea' in the Tang Dynasty already mentioning 'Hunan tea'. During the Ming and Qing dynasties, Yiyang served as a key hub on the Tea Horse Road, exporting dark tea to the northwest. In 1958, the Yiyang Tea Factory was established, specializing in Fu brick tea production and becoming a designated enterprise for border trade tea. Xiangyi inherits the craftsmanship of the Yiyang Tea Factory, excelling in the 'flowering' technique that cultivates abundant golden flowers (Eurotium cristatum), imparting a pure fungal aroma. Culturally, Xiangyi embodies the Hunan tea spirit of 'tea as a vehicle for the Way', blending traditional methods with modern technology to preserve the millennium-old heritage of dark tea.

Cultural Note

The production of Xiangyi Fu brick tea involves processes such as piling, fermentation, pressing, and 'flowering' (fa hua), with the latter being the core step. By controlling temperature and humidity, the growth of Eurotium cristatum is encouraged, forming the distinctive golden flowers. This technique, rooted in ancient methods, is now integrated with modern biotechnology to ensure consistent quality. Xiangyi upholds both tradition and innovation, promoting the culture of dark tea.
