Brand Story

Diyang was born in the 1930s, amid the surge of the National Products Movement. In 1932, Tianjin East Asia Worsted Textile Company (predecessor of today's Tianjin Textile Group) launched this domestic wool brand to break the monopoly of foreign goods. The name 'Diyang' means 'resisting foreign goods and revitalizing national products.' Founder Song Feiqing, driven by the ideal of industrial salvation, introduced advanced equipment and techniques to produce wool of quality comparable to imports. With the rallying cry 'Chinese use Chinese goods,' Diyang quickly won the market and became a banner of national industry. Its logo, two rams butting heads, symbolizes the competition between domestic and foreign products, inspiring self-improvement. More than a product, Diyang carries the fighting spirit and cultural memory of modern Chinese industry, witnessing Tianjin's glorious history as a northern industrial hub.

“Chinese use Chinese goods; Diyang revitalizes China.”

Cultural Note

Diyang combines traditional wool spinning techniques with modern industrial methods, creating a unique production process. Its brand culture emphasizes national self-strengthening and the consciousness of domestic goods, epitomizing the development of modern Chinese industry and commerce. The logo, two rams in confrontation, signifies both competition and unity, reflecting the Chinese philosophy of 'harmony in diversity.'
