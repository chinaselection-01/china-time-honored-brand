Brand Story

Founded in the late Qing Dynasty, Demao Gong's exact founding year is unverified, but it is said to have originated from a small pastry shop in Xi'an. During the late Qing and early Republic period, a shopkeeper surnamed Li (full name unknown) refined traditional flaky pastry techniques, creating the crystal cake with a crisp crust and soft, translucent filling. Its unique texture quickly gained popularity. In the Republican era, Demao Gong became a favored gift among Xi'an's elite, with its shop located near the Bell Tower (current site unverified). The name 'Demao Gong' reflects the merchant's virtues: 'De' (virtue) as the foundation of business, 'Mao' (prosperity) for flourishing, and 'Gong' (respect) for hospitality, embodying the integrity and elegance of Shaanxi merchants. After the founding of New China, Demao Gong underwent public-private partnership, preserving its craft. In 2006, its crystal cake-making technique was listed as a Shaanxi provincial intangible cultural heritage, making it a living fossil of Guanzhong food culture. Although not officially recognized as a 'China Time-honored Brand,' it enjoys deep folk acclaim and is synonymous with 'pastry master' in Xi'an.

Cultural Note

The craft of Demao Gong's crystal cake emphasizes 'crisp crust, soft filling, and distinct layers,' involving over ten steps such as kneading dough, making pastry, filling, and baking, all requiring the master's feel for heat. The filling uses sugar, red and green preserved fruits, walnuts, and rose jam, reflecting the local produce of the Guanzhong region. The brand carries the urban memory of Xi'an and is an indispensable symbol in festive customs.
