Brand Story

Sanhuan traces its roots to Shandong, a region historically celebrated for its metal forging expertise. During the Ming and Qing dynasties, Shandong artisans crafted renowned knives and scissors with exceptional skill. Inheriting this legacy, Sanhuan combines traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to produce high-quality hardware and cutlery. The brand name 'Sanhuan' (three rings) symbolizes the interconnected values of quality, credibility, and service. While the exact founding year and founder remain unrecorded, every Sanhuan product embodies the wisdom and dedication of Shandong artisans, reflecting the unwavering pursuit of excellence characteristic of China's time-honored brands. More than just tools, Sanhuan represents the cultural heritage of Shandong's hardware industry, carrying forward regional history and the spirit of craftsmanship.
