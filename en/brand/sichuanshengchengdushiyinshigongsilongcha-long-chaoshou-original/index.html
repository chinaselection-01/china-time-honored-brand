Brand Story

The Long Chaoshao Head Store, located in Chengdu, Sichuan, traces its origins to the 1940s during the Republic of China era. At that time, Chengdu's catering industry was thriving. Founder Zhang Guangwu and his partners opened a small shop near the commercial area, specializing in chaoshao (wonton). As the shop was close to the 'Nonghua Tea House', they adopted the homophone 'Long' (dragon) from 'Nong' to symbolize auspiciousness, thus naming it 'Long Chaoshao'. From the outset, the shop emphasized quality ingredients and craftsmanship, using pork leg meat, eggs, and specially made wrappers, with a broth slow-cooked from chicken, duck, and pork bones, creating a unique flavor of thin wrappers, tender filling, and savory soup. Long Chaoshao quickly gained a stellar reputation and became a standout among Chengdu snacks. Culturally, it is not just a food but a microcosm of Chengdu's leisurely dining culture, reflecting the Sichuan people's philosophy of pursuing authentic flavors and practicality. Over decades, the Long Chaoshao Head Store has become a symbol of Chengdu snacks, with its craftsmanship passed down through generations, preserving traditional tastes while adapting to modern needs, continuing to serve diners from all over.

Cultural Note

The craft of Long Chaoshao emphasizes 'thin wrappers, tender filling, and savory broth'. The wrappers are hand-rolled, the filling is made from selected pork leg meat, and the broth is simmered with chicken, duck, and pork bones. Its seasoning balances savory and slightly spicy, reflecting the diverse flavors of Sichuan cuisine. As a representative Chengdu snack, Long Chaoshao embodies the city's street food culture, witnessing the evolution of Chengdu's catering industry. Its business philosophy and production techniques hold ethnographic value.
