Brand Story

Mayinglong was founded around 1582 during the Wanli era of the Ming Dynasty by Ma Jintang, a native of Dingzhou, Hebei, who opened a pharmacy in the capital to develop eye medicines. His formulas blended various Chinese herbs with distinctive processing methods, gaining renown for their efficacy. In the early Qing Dynasty, descendant Ma Yinglong inherited the family business, moved the pharmacy to Beijing, and named it after himself, establishing the Mayinglong brand. During the Tongzhi era, Ma Wanxing expanded southward, opening a branch in Wuhan and spreading the brand's influence across the Yangtze River basin. The eye medicine, known as 'Dingzhou Eye Medicine,' was crafted with a core 'Eight Treasures' formula, using carefully selected ingredients and meticulous preparation. In the Republican era, descendant Ma Qishan officially renamed the business 'Mayinglong Pharmaceutical Factory' and gradually developed anorectal products, forging a unique path of 'starting with eye medicine and thriving in anorectal care.' Carrying centuries of traditional Chinese medicine culture, Mayinglong's 'Eight Treasures' formula and manufacturing techniques are listed as national intangible cultural heritage, embodying the wisdom and legacy of traditional medicine.

Cultural Note

Mayinglong's 'Eight Treasures' formula, originating in the Ming Dynasty, comprises eight precious ingredients such as musk, bezoar, amber, and pearl. The preparation involves dozens of steps including grinding, compounding, and mixing, with great emphasis on 'heat control' and 'fineness.' In 2011, these techniques were inscribed on the National Intangible Cultural Heritage list, reflecting the craftsman spirit of traditional Chinese medicine: 'Though no one sees the preparation, heaven knows the integrity.'
