Brand Story

Gong Qian originates from Sichuan, a region renowned for its vibrant handicraft traditions. Although the exact founding year is not officially documented, the craft lineage can be traced back to the Ming and Qing dynasties. According to local chronicles, the Gong family has been engaged in craftsmanship for generations, earning a reputation for exceptional skill. During the Qianlong reign (1736-1795), an ancestor established a workshop in Chengdu, focusing on lacquerware and bamboo weaving. Their works, known for their delicacy and refinement, were highly appreciated by literati and scholars. In the Republican era (1912-1949), the Gong Qian brand gradually took shape, blending traditional techniques with contemporary aesthetics, and its products gained popularity beyond the province. After the founding of the People's Republic of China, the craft was preserved and later revitalized following the reform and opening-up. The brand is named after its founder, Gong Qian, who inherited the family's skills and dedicated herself to promoting Sichuan's traditional crafts. Her works have been exhibited and awarded, making the brand a cultural emblem of the region. Gong Qian not only represents the continuity of craftsmanship but also embodies the profound cultural heritage of Sichuan, integrating local natural materials with humanistic spirit, reflecting a philosophy of harmony between humans and nature.

Cultural Note

Gong Qian's craftsmanship emphasizes material selection and handwork, adhering to traditional techniques. For instance, lacquerware polishing and bamboo basket weaving each require dozens of steps. Skills are passed down through an apprenticeship system, emphasizing the unity of mind and hand. The works embody Sichuan's aesthetic sensibilities and the wisdom of daily life.
