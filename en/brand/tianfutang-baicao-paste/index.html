Brand Story

Tianfutang, originating from Zhejiang, derives its name from the auspicious meaning 'Heaven-sent blessings, healing the world with wellness.' Although the exact founding year is not officially verified, local chronicles and oral traditions trace its roots to the mid-Qing Dynasty, around 1800 AD, when Zhejiang's pharmaceutical industry flourished. The founder, whose surname awaits confirmation, was well-versed in herbal medicine and adhered to the principles of 'selecting premium ingredients and refining with precision.' The 'Fuzi' brand of patent medicines gained a reputation for their efficacy. By the late Qing and early Republic era, Tianfutang had grown considerably, standing alongside other Hangzhou pharmacies, with products like 'Baicao Ointment' and 'Yangyuan Pills' sold as far as Shanghai and Jiangsu. Culturally, Tianfutang integrates the medical traditions of Wu and Yue, emphasizing seasonal wellness. Each winter solstice, it hosts a 'Tonic Festival' to promote medicinal cuisine. Despite wars and changes, Tianfutang's spirit of 'benevolent healing' endures, making it a vital symbol of Zhejiang's traditional Chinese medicine culture.

Cultural Note

Tianfutang upholds the craft of 'authentic herbs and ancient preparation methods,' including the 'nine-steam-nine-sun' process and the motto 'Though preparation is unseen, heaven knows the heart,' reflecting Zhejiang merchants' integrity and the TCM principle of preventive care. The annual winter solstice tonic festival, blending medicinal cuisine and folk customs, exemplifies the living heritage of Zhejiang's TCM culture.
