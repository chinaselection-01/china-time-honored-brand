Cultural Note

The brewing process follows ancient methods, including soaking rice, steaming, cooling, mixing with starter, fermenting, pressing, and heating the wine, with careful attention to season and humidity. The starter is made from local herbs, imparting a unique flavor. Culturally, the wine pairs exquisitely with Cantonese cuisine, reflecting the philosophy of 'harmony in diversity,' and is integrated into folk customs such as ancestral rites and weddings.
