Brand Story

Lushan Yunwu Tea Farm, perched on the scenic peaks of Lushan Mountain, traces its roots to the Tang Dynasty (618-907 AD), when Lushan tea was already presented as tribute to the imperial court. During the Song Dynasty (960-1279), Lushan Yunwu tea developed its distinctive character, celebrated by poets and scholars. In the Ming Dynasty (1368-1644), it was designated as a tribute tea, gaining widespread fame. In modern times, the farm has blended traditional techniques with contemporary technology to preserve and promote this precious heritage. Blessed by the mountain's misty environment and crystal springs, our tea leaves are plump, tender, and covered with fine white hairs, yielding a clear infusion with a delicate aroma and mellow taste. We adhere to traditional hand-picking and crafting, infusing each leaf with the artisan's dedication and reverence for nature.

“Misty mountains, timeless tea; crafted with heart, fragrant for ages.”

Cultural Note

The crafting of Lushan Yunwu tea involves precise techniques such as 'shaking, carrying, pressing, tossing, lifting, spreading, grasping, pressing, and grinding' (dou, dai, ji, shuai, ting, tuo, zhua, ya, mo), with meticulous control of heat, preserving the essence of traditional handcrafted tea. The farm practices ecological cultivation, using organic fertilizers to ensure the tea's natural purity. Lushan Yunwu tea is not merely a beverage; it is a symbol of Lushan culture, embodying the wisdom and emotions of generations of tea artisans.
