Brand Story

Dong'e, located in Dong'e County, Liaocheng, Shandong, has been famous for producing Ejiao since ancient times. According to the Compendium of Materia Medica, Ejiao dates back to the Qin and Han dynasties and flourished by the Tang Dynasty. Legend has it that the Langxi River in Dong'e County, with its sweet, mineral-rich waters, combined with local black donkey hides, produced a gelatin of exceptional purity and efficacy. Renowned physicians such as Zhang Zhongjing and Sun Simiao praised Ejiao as a superior tonic. During the Ming and Qing dynasties, Dong'e Ejiao became a tribute to the imperial court, spreading its fame far and wide. In modern times, Dong'e has innovated by integrating traditional methods with contemporary technology. Its production techniques have been inscribed on the National Intangible Cultural Heritage list, reflecting its profound cultural heritage. Dong'e is not merely a product but a symbol of traditional Chinese medicine, carrying the auspicious meaning of health and longevity.

Cultural Note

The art of making Dong'e Ejiao originated in the Tang Dynasty and has been passed down through millennia. The traditional process involves soaking hides, scraping hair, blanching, dissolving, filtering, concentrating, gelling, and cutting. The key lies in Dong'e's unique groundwater, rich in minerals like calcium and magnesium, which aids in the purification of the gelatin. In 2008, the craft was listed as a National Intangible Cultural Heritage, exemplifying the essence of traditional Chinese medicine.
