Brand Story

Yicui, rooted in Jiangsu, draws from the province's long-standing tradition of arts and crafts. During the Ming and Qing dynasties, Jiangnan's handicrafts flourished, with Suzhou embroidery, Yangzhou lacquerware, and Yixing purple clay teapots gaining fame. The brand name, meaning 'gathering of arts,' embodies the spirit of uniting Jiangsu's finest techniques and passing down centuries-old craftsmanship. Though its founding year and founder are not officially recorded, it is said that early artisans came from local families who lived by their skills, blending family secrets with contemporary aesthetics. Yicui's products not only showcase the delicacy and elegance of Jiangsu craftsmanship but also carry the profound cultural heritage of the region. Today, Yicui is dedicated to merging traditional techniques with modern design, revitalizing ancient crafts and serving as a bridge between history and the future.

Cultural Note

Yicui's techniques are inherited from Jiangsu's intangible cultural heritage, such as Suzhou embroidery and Yixing purple clay teapot making. The brand insists on handmade production, paying attention to every detail, with each piece embodying the artisan's dedication. Culturally, Yicui reflects the aesthetic taste of Jiangnan literati, pursuing a natural, subtle, and refined beauty. The brand also actively participates in the preservation and transmission of intangible cultural heritage, bringing traditional crafts into modern life through innovative design.
