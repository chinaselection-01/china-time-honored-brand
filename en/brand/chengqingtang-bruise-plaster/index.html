Brand Story

Chengqingtang, originating from Anhui, derives its name from the auspicious phrase 'Chengqing Yanfu' (inheriting blessings and extending fortune), embodying a deep heritage of family medical practice. Legend has it that during the Guangxu reign of the Qing Dynasty (1875-1908), a renowned local physician named Wang, well-versed in herbal medicine, practiced medicine to aid the community. To better serve his neighbors, he opened a pharmacy in Luzhou Prefecture (modern-day Hefei), naming it 'Chengqing' with the hope that his medical skills and benevolence would be passed down through generations. Wang specialized in treating injuries, fractures, and rheumatic ailments, and his self-made plasters and medicinal wines gained fame for their efficacy. During the Republic of China era (1912-1949), the third-generation inheritor, Wang Jinghe, expanded the business by incorporating effective prescriptions and refining processing techniques, enhancing the purity of the medicines. He established the motto 'Integrity first, genuine medicines at fair prices,' earning the trust of the public. Chengqingtang was not merely a pharmacy but also embodied the Huizhou merchants' cultural spirit of 'being merchants yet embracing Confucianism.' Its pharmaceutical techniques integrated traditional Chinese medical theories with local medicinal practices, reflecting the profound medical culture of the Huizhou region. Through over a century of changes, Chengqingtang has remained true to its original mission of 'inheriting ancient methods and celebrating well-being,' making its story a vivid epitome of Huizhou's medical heritage.

Cultural Note

Chengqingtang's pharmaceutical techniques adhere to the principle of 'processing according to ancient methods,' emphasizing the authenticity of medicinal materials and precise control of processing heat. The production of its plasters involves multiple steps such as soaking, boiling, filtering, and collecting, reflecting the essence of traditional Chinese materia medica processing. The brand culture integrates the Huizhou merchants' integrity-based business philosophy with the TCM concept of 'preventive treatment,' highlighting the healer's benevolence and serving as an important carrier of Anhui's local medical culture.
