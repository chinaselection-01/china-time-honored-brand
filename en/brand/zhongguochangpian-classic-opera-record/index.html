Brand Story

The history of China Records can be traced back to the early 20th century. In 1908, a Frenchman founded Pathé Records in Shanghai, marking the beginning of China's record industry. In 1917, at the invitation of Sun Yat-sen, a Japanese entrepreneur established the Great China Record Factory, which later ceased operations but resumed in 1920, becoming the first Chinese-owned record company. In 1930, the Shanghai Pathé company built China's first recording studio, laying the technical foundation for record production. After the founding of the People's Republic of China in 1949, the People's Record Factory was established in Beijing in 1950, later renamed China Record Factory, to unify national record publishing. In 1958, the China Record Society was formally established, marking an organized phase of development. China Records has witnessed the evolution of modern Chinese music, opera, and literature, preserving a vast archive of precious audio materials.

Cultural Note

China Records' craftsmanship integrates traditional techniques such as recording, groove cutting, and pressing, with particular expertise in analog recording and vinyl pressing. Its cultural significance lies in preserving China's sound heritage since the 20th century, including opera, folk arts, folk songs, and revolutionary songs, making it a vital medium for studying modern Chinese culture.

What is a "China Time-Honored Brand" (中华老字号)?

A China Time-Honored Brand is a designation by China’s Ministry of Commerce for brands with a long history that inherit products, craftsmanship or services across generations and have operated continuously to the present.
