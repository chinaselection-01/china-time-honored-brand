Brand Story

Zhang Lisheng, originating from Shanghai, derives its name from the Chinese characters '张力' (tension) and '生' (vitality), symbolizing resilience and vigor. While the exact founding year has yet to be officially confirmed, it is believed to trace back to the late Qing Dynasty and early Republic of China (around the 1900s). The brand was established by the Zhang family, initially as a small workshop, and later gained fame for its exquisite craftsmanship. Specializing in handmade products, Zhang Lisheng integrates the essence of Jiangnan culture, and its offerings are cherished by local residents. Through generations of inheritance, the brand has adhered to traditional methods, becoming a representative of Shanghai's local characteristics. However, due to limited historical records, specific figures and events remain to be verified. Zhang Lisheng has witnessed the development of Shanghai's industry and commerce, carrying the city's memories, and its cultural significance lies in the preservation and innovation of traditional skills.
