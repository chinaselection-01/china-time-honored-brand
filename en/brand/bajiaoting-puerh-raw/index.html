Brand Story

Originating from Xishuangbanna, Yunnan, the Bajiaoting brand takes its name from the octagonal pavilions found in local Dai villages, symbolizing good fortune and unity. Established in the 1980s, during China's reform and opening-up, the brand capitalized on the resurgence of the Pu'er tea market. Leveraging the high-quality large-leaf tea resources of Menghai, the founder adhered to traditional sun-drying techniques to craft Pu'er teas with distinctive aged aroma and mellow taste. Bajiaoting has since grown, blending quality with culture, earning acclaim both domestically and internationally. It stands as a representative brand of Yunnan Pu'er tea, emphasizing nature, health, and harmony, reflecting the profound tea traditions of Yunnan's ethnic minorities. Despite the exact founding year awaiting official confirmation, Bajiaoting has established a solid reputation in the tea industry through consistent quality and distinctive cultural character.

Cultural Note

Bajiaoting upholds traditional Yunnan Pu'er tea-making techniques, including sun-drying, rolling, and fermentation, with an emphasis on natural aging. The brand is committed to ecological protection, collaborating with local tea farmers to promote sustainable harvesting. Its cultural essence integrates the tea customs of the Dai, Hani, and other ethnic minorities, echoing the historical memory of the Ancient Tea Horse Road.
