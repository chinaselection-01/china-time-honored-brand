Brand Story

The name Siming originates from the ancient name of Ningbo, Siming Mountain. Its history dates back to the mid-Qing Dynasty when Ningbo, as a major trading port on the southeast coast, thrived in the herbal medicine industry. According to local chronicles, during the Jiaqing reign (1796-1820), Ningbo medicine merchants established a pharmacy in the city under the name 'Siming', dealing in authentic herbs and various traditional preparations. At that time, the Ningbo medicine guild rose alongside the 'Ningbo Bang' merchants. Leveraging the abundant herbal resources of the eastern Zhejiang mountains, Siming selected high-quality ingredients such as Baizhu (Atractylodes) and Zhe Beimu (Fritillaria thunbergii), and adhered to rigorous processing techniques, gradually earning a good reputation. By the Guangxu reign (1875-1908), Siming had expanded significantly, with its self-made 'Siming Dan' and 'Baohe Wan' selling well across eastern Zhejiang and even reaching Shanghai through the Ningbo merchant network. During the Republic of China period (1912-1949), Siming continued its traditional methods and participated in local charitable medicine distribution. Siming is not just a pharmacy; it embodies the cultural spirit of the Ningbo merchants—integrity in business and benefiting the people—reflecting the fusion of eastern Zhejiang's traditional Chinese medicine and Ningbo's regional culture.

Cultural Note

Siming adheres to the principle of 'preparing medicines according to ancient methods', using authentic eastern Zhejiang herbs such as Baizhu and Zhe Beimu, and employing traditional techniques like soaking, stir-frying, roasting, and calcining. Its business philosophy integrates the integrity and pragmatism of the Ningbo merchants, emphasizing quality and efficacy, while also engaging in charitable medicine distribution, reflecting the TCM tradition of 'benevolence and healing'.
