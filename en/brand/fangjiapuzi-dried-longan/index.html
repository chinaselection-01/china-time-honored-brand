Brand Story

Fang Jia Pu Zi traces its roots back to the late Qing Dynasty (around the late 19th century) in Fujian, where the Fang ancestors started a small business selling dried goods and traditional pastries. Their commitment to quality and honest dealing quickly earned them a loyal local following. In the early years of the Republic of China (around 1912), the second-generation heir, Fang Wenshan (name to be verified), officially established the brand 'Fang Jia Pu Zi', specializing in Fujian specialties like dried longan and dried lychee. He pioneered the 'three-steam, three-sun' technique for processing longan, resulting in a golden color and a sweet, non-cloying taste that won widespread acclaim. More than just a shop, Fang Jia Pu Zi represents the culinary culture and business wisdom of Fujian, with its motto 'Genuine goods at fair prices, honest to all' influencing generations of merchants. Over a century later, the brand remains dedicated to traditional methods while embracing modern management, serving as a cultural bridge between past and present.

Cultural Note

Fang Jia Pu Zi upholds traditional food processing techniques, such as the 'three-steam, three-sun' method for dried longan, which requires precise steaming and sun-drying to ensure quality. The brand's emphasis on integrity and trust, along with its focus on sourcing authentic ingredients and maintaining hygiene, reflects the Min merchants' spirit of 'daring to strive' and 'honesty as the foundation'. These skills and cultural values are integral to Fujian's culinary heritage.
