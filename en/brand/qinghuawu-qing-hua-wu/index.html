Brand Story

The history of Qinghuawu liquor dates back to the Tang Dynasty (618-907 AD). Qinghua Town in Wuyuan, blessed with crystal-clear spring water, has been an ideal place for brewing since ancient times. During the Song Dynasty (960-1279 AD), the local brewing industry flourished, and the 'Wu liquor' produced here gained fame across the region. In the Ming and Qing dynasties (1368-1911 AD), Qinghuawu was listed as a tribute to the imperial court, and its brewing techniques were passed down through generations. In modern times, the Qinghuawu Distillery has combined traditional methods with modern technology, revitalizing this ancient liquor. The brand name 'Qinghua' derives from the town's name, symbolizing 'elegance and nobility,' reflecting the beauty of Wuyuan's landscapes and the profound cultural heritage of its liquor. Qinghuawu is not only a source of pride for the people of Wuyuan but also a shining pearl in China's liquor culture, carrying a unique memory of history and regional identity.

Cultural Note

The brewing technique of Qinghuawu originated in the Tang Dynasty and has been passed down for over a millennium, integrating the local water, soil, and culture of Wuyuan. The process strictly follows ancient methods, including grain selection, starter making, fermentation, distillation, and aging. Each step embodies the craftsmanship and dedication of the brewers. The liquor is clear and transparent, with an elegant aroma and a mellow taste, offering a unique regional flavor. It is an important part of Wuyuan's intangible cultural heritage.
