Brand Story

Founded in early 20th-century Shanghai, Guan Sheng Yuan's exact founding year is unverified, but it is commonly traced to 1918. Its founder, Xian Guansheng (1887-1952), originally named Xian Bingsheng, hailed from Foshan, Guangdong. Starting with a small food stall, he opened the Guan Sheng Yuan food shop in Shanghai's southern district, selling candies and preserved fruits. Emphasizing quality and innovation, he adopted modern business practices, propelling rapid growth. By the 1920s, the brand had a flagship store on Nanjing Road, becoming a household name. In the 1930s, it introduced Cantonese-style mooncakes, celebrated for their thin crusts and rich fillings. During the war, it continued production to support the rear. In 1956, it underwent public-private partnership, evolving into today's Shanghai Guan Sheng Yuan Food Co., Ltd. The brand represents Shanghai's modern food industry and the fusion of Lingnan and Shanghai culinary traditions.

Cultural Note

Guan Sheng Yuan's craftsmanship merges Cantonese pastry artistry with Shanghai flavors, emphasizing premium ingredients and meticulous techniques. Mooncake-making involves multiple steps to perfect the crust-to-filling ratio. Its brand culture reflects Shanghai's entrepreneurial spirit and the aesthetic of East-meets-West cuisine.
