Brand Story

Foci was founded in 1929 (the 18th year of the Republic of China) in Shanghai by patriotic industrialist Yu Huiguan. Distressed by the inconvenience and imprecision of traditional herbal decoctions, Yu introduced Western pharmaceutical techniques to create the first concentrated herbal pills, championing the concept of 'scientific extraction and improved traditional medicine.' This innovation not only revolutionized herbal preparation but also advanced the modernization of Chinese medicine. After relocating to Lanzhou during the war, Foci took root in the northwest, leveraging Gansu's authentic medicinal herbs and combining traditional processing with modern technology to develop its unique 'Foci pharmaceutical craftsmanship.' Its products are now exported worldwide, serving as a vital vehicle for spreading Chinese medicine culture. Foci's journey embodies the resilience and innovation of modern Chinese industry and witnesses the transformation of traditional medicine into a modern discipline.
