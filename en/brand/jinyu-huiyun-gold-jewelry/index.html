Brand Story

Jinyu, a distinguished jewelry brand from Anhui, has a history that dates back to the Ming and Qing dynasties, when the region was renowned for its exquisite gold and silver craftsmanship. According to legend, during the Qianlong reign (1736-1795), skilled artisans in Huizhou established the Jinyu workshop, specializing in gold and silver ornaments that integrated Huizhou-style carving and inlay techniques. During the Republic of China era (1912-1949), Jinyu expanded with shops in Wuhu and Anqing, earning a reputation for integrity and quality. After the founding of the People's Republic of China, Jinyu's craftsmanship continued to thrive, and today it stands as a leading jewelry brand in Anhui. The brand embodies the spirit of Huizhou merchants, preserving traditional jewelry-making techniques while infusing regional culture into modern designs.

Cultural Note

Jinyu carries forward the Huizhou gold and silver filigree craftsmanship, emphasizing meticulous handwork and the integration of Hui culture. Its techniques include filigree (huasi), engraving (zanke), and inlay, reflecting the spirit of traditional artisans. The brand selects premium materials and designs that balance classical and contemporary aesthetics, dedicated to promoting Chinese jewelry culture.
