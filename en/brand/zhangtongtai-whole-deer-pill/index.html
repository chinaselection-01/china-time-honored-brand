Brand Story

Founded in 1805 during the Qing Dynasty by Ningbo merchant Zhang Mei, Zhang Tongtai Pharmacy began as 'Zhang Tongtai Tang' on Xue'erhe Road in Hangzhou. Adhering to the principle of 'genuine goods at fair prices, honest with all', Zhang Mei dealt in authentic medicinal materials, gradually expanding the business. During the Tongzhi era (1862-1874), his grandson Zhang Lu'an inherited the enterprise, expanded the shop, recruited renowned physicians, and refined the production of pills, powders, ointments, and elixirs, elevating the pharmacy's reputation. Zhang Lu'an placed great emphasis on herb quality, personally sourcing materials and improving processing techniques. His 'Quanlu Pills' and 'Renshen Zaizao Pills' became famous across the Jiangnan region. During the Republican era, Zhang Tongtai, along with Hu Qingyutang and Ye Zhongdetang, were hailed as the three major pharmacies of Hangzhou. The pharmacy's architecture, a blend of Chinese and Western styles with ornate carvings, is now a city-level cultural relic. Beyond selling medicines, Zhang Tongtai also provided free medical consultations and donated medicines during epidemics, earning deep public trust. Its legacy of 'ancient processing methods' and the philosophy of 'healing and nurturing life' exemplify the integration of Zhejiang merchants' integrity and traditional Chinese medicine culture.

Cultural Note

Zhang Tongtai adheres to the ancestral precept of 'ancient processing methods', using authentic herbs and techniques such as soaking, stir-frying, roasting, and calcining, with particular renown for 'nine-steaming and nine-sunning'. The meticulous preparation of pills, with careful attention to heat and timing, reflects the TCM concept of 'harmony between heaven and humanity'. The pharmacy's hall bears a plaque reading 'Ji Shi' (benefiting the world), embodying the benevolent spirit of a healer and exerting a significant influence on the inheritance of Jiangnan's traditional Chinese medicine culture.
