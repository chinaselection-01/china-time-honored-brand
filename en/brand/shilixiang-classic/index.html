Brand Story

Shilixiang liquor originates from Hebei Province, with a brewing history that can be traced back to the Ming and Qing dynasties. According to local chronicles, the region's liquor industry was already well-established during the Ming Dynasty and reached its peak during the Qianlong era (1736-1795) of the Qing Dynasty. Legend has it that Emperor Qianlong, during his southern inspection tours, tasted this liquor and praised it for its fragrance that could be smelled from ten li away, thus giving it the name 'Shilixiang' (literally 'ten-li fragrance'). However, this legend lacks direct historical evidence and remains unverified. In modern times, the Shilixiang distillery has continuously improved upon traditional techniques, developing a unique strong-aroma style. The brewing craft, passed down through generations, integrates the local water, soil, and climate, making it an important representative of Hebei's liquor culture. Although the exact founding year of the brand remains undetermined, its profound cultural heritage and exquisite brewing skills have earned it a prestigious reputation among regional spirits.

Cultural Note

Shilixiang liquor employs traditional solid-state fermentation, using sorghum and wheat as raw materials. Combined with the region's unique microbial environment, it achieves a style characterized by rich cellar aroma and a sweet, refreshing taste. Its brewing technique has been listed as an intangible cultural heritage of Hebei Province, exemplifying the typical characteristics of northern baijiu.
