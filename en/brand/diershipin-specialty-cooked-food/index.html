Brand Story

Dier Food traces its roots to early 20th-century Shanghai, evolving from a grocery store during the Republican era. As Shanghai's commerce flourished in the 1920s, Dier Food emerged as one of the city's notable food shops. It has witnessed Shanghai's journey from a treaty port to a modern metropolis, navigating key historical milestones such as public-private partnership and reform and opening-up. During the planned economy, it served as a state-owned store providing daily necessities; in the market economy era, it innovated by introducing premium foods from across the country, becoming a trusted food landmark for Shanghainese. Throughout its history, model workers and master craftsmen have passed down exquisite food processing and appraisal skills, infusing every product with the refinement and inclusiveness of Haipai cuisine. Dier Food is not just a store; it is a microcosm of Shanghai's urban culture, carrying the taste memories of generations.

Cultural Note

Dier Food's craftsmanship emphasizes a blend of tradition and innovation, particularly in its cooked delicacies and pastries, where meticulous ingredient selection and precise cooking techniques are paramount. The recipes and methods passed down orally from master to apprentice preserve the authentic old Shanghai flavors. Meanwhile, the store actively adopts modern food safety management to ensure quality. This commitment to traditional skills and adherence to modern standards constitute its unique cultural essence.
