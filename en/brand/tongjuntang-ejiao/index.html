Brand Story

Tongjunting traces its origins to the legendary medicine sage Tong Jun, who, according to ancient lore, gathered herbs and practiced medicine in Tonglu County, Zhejiang, earning a lasting reputation for his benevolence. To honor his legacy, later generations established Tongjunting in Zhejiang, dedicating themselves to promoting traditional Chinese medicine. Over centuries, the brand has adhered to the principle of 'authentic herbs and benevolent practice,' integrating traditional processing techniques with clinical experience to form a unique medical system. During its heyday, Tongjunting's physicians not only refined prescriptions but also excelled in herb identification and processing, producing decoction pieces renowned for their quality and efficacy, earning the deep trust of the people. More than a medical institution, Tongjunting serves as a vital carrier of TCM culture, its ethical standards and skills profoundly influencing the development of traditional medicine. Today, Tongjunting continues to innovate, breathing new life into ancient wisdom.
