Brand Story

Originating from Jiangsu, the Ma Xiangxing brand traces its history back to the Qing Dynasty (1644-1912), though the exact founding year remains unverified. It is said that the Ma family, generation after generation, devoted themselves to jewelry making, gaining renown for their superb skills. During the late Qing and early Republic period, Ma Xiangxing gradually rose to prominence in Jiangsu, becoming a prestigious jewelry house through a blend of adherence to tradition and innovative spirit. The name 'Ma Xiangxing' signifies auspiciousness and prosperity, reflecting the Chinese cultural aspiration for a better life. The brand's creations merge the elegance of Jiangnan water towns with the essence of classical culture, favored by scholars, literati, and prominent merchants. Each piece, crafted with meticulous attention to material selection and technique, embodies the artisans' dedication and wisdom. Ma Xiangxing is not merely a brand but a microcosm of traditional Chinese jewelry culture, witnessing the flourishing of commerce and craftsmanship in Jiangsu. Despite limited historical records, it maintains a mysterious and noble image through oral tradition, holding profound cultural significance worthy of study and preservation.

Cultural Note

Ma Xiangxing's jewelry craftsmanship inherits the folk techniques of Jiangnan from the Qing Dynasty, emphasizing meticulous hand-carving and the harmony of natural materials and design. Its cultural essence blends the Confucian tradition of jade with Jiangnan's aesthetic sensibilities, reflecting the refinement and elegance of Chinese jewelry culture. Although not officially listed as intangible cultural heritage, its craftsmanship and style are representative in folk traditions, serving as an important reference for the study of Jiangnan handicraft history.
