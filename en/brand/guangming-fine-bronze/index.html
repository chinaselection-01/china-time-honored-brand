Brand Story

Guangming, a representative craft brand in Shanghai, traces its origins to the flourishing handicraft industry that emerged after the opening of the port in 1843 (the 23rd year of Daoguang's reign). The convergence of Western techniques and local traditions gave rise to the unique Haipai style. While the brand's exact founding year is not officially documented, its craft lineage is closely intertwined with the city's development. In the early 20th century (from the Guangxu era to the early Republic), a group of artisans gathered in Shanghai, serving residents with their superb skills and gradually forming the predecessor of the Guangming brand. These craftsmen, though largely unnamed, passed down their expertise in metalworking and woodworking through oral instruction and hands-on apprenticeship. Guangming's cultural significance lies in its embodiment of the Haipai spirit—'meticulous craftsmanship and inclusive innovation.' Its products, widely used in home furnishings and decorations, were cherished by locals and have become an integral part of Shanghai's collective memory.

Cultural Note

Guangming's craftsmanship is rooted in the multicultural soil of post-opening Shanghai, blending Chinese and Western techniques with a focus on both practicality and aesthetics. Each piece is meticulously handcrafted, with every step strictly controlled, reflecting the Haipai pursuit of perfection. Guangming's skills not only preserve the city's memory but also epitomize the transformation of modern Chinese handicraft industries.
