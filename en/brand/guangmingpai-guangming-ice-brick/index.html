Brand Story

Guangming was born in Shanghai in the early 1950s, a time when the newly founded People's Republic of China was rebuilding. In 1950, the Shanghai Municipal Government decided to develop the food industry to improve citizens' lives, and Yimin Food Factory No.1 was tasked with producing ice cream and canned goods. Under the leadership of factory director Jia Bingcan, who later became Minister of Light Industry, the 'Guangming' brand ice cream was officially launched in 1951, symbolizing 'bright future' and hope for the new society. Guangming quickly became the leader in Shanghai's ice cream market, with its iconic red packaging and 'Guangming' logo winning hearts. Over the years, Guangming expanded its product line to include dairy, candies, and more, becoming a symbol of quality and sentiment for Shanghainese. Its story reflects the revitalization of national industry and the contribution of state-owned enterprises to people's livelihoods, making it an integral part of Shanghai's urban memory.

Cultural Note

Guangming's craftsmanship inherits the essence of Shanghai-style food, emphasizing fresh ingredients and delicate textures. Its ice cream combines traditional recipes with modern technology, preserving the old Shanghai flavor. The brand culture embodies the state-owned enterprise's commitment to quality and care for citizens' lives, making it a part of Shanghai's culinary culture.
