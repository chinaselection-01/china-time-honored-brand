Brand Story

Founded in 1865 during the Tongzhi era of the Qing Dynasty, Bian Fumao began as a humble shoe stall in Qinghefang, Hangzhou, established by the Bian family. The founder, Bian Fumao, dedicated himself to shoemaking, emphasizing quality materials and meticulous craftsmanship. His cloth shoes, known for their comfort and durability, quickly gained a loyal following. During the Republic of China era, Bian Fumao became a celebrated shoe store in Hangzhou, with products sold across Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Shanghai, beloved by both urbanites and literati. The shoemaking techniques blend traditional Jiangnan handcraft, featuring the intricate 'thousand-layer sole' (qiancengdi) and fine stitching, showcasing the wisdom of folk artisans. Bian Fumao is not just a commercial brand but a microcosm of Hangzhou's folk culture, witnessing the rise and fall of modern handicraft industry. Today, Bian Fumao continues its traditional craft, standing as a living heritage among China's time-honored brands.

Cultural Note

Bian Fumao's shoemaking is distinguished by its 'thousand-layer sole' (qiancengdi), crafted from high-quality cotton cloth and hemp thread through dozens of steps including pasting, cutting, sole-stitching, and upper-attaching. The soles are thick and durable, while the uppers remain crisp and neat. The craft emphasizes 'every stitch counts,' reflecting the rigor and patience of Jiangnan artisans, and represents the essence of traditional cloth shoe culture.
