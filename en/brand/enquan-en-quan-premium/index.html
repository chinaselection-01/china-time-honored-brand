Brand Story

Originating in Jiangxi, the Enquan brand traces its roots back to the Guangxu reign of the Qing Dynasty (1875-1908), when it was founded by a local sauce master. Using carefully selected soybeans and premium wheat, the founder employed traditional brewing techniques to create soy sauce and fermented black beans with distinctive flavors, quickly gaining a reputation across the region. During the Republic of China era (1912-1949), Enquan products were presented as local delicacies to government officials and sold to neighboring provinces. After the founding of the People's Republic of China, the Enquan sauce workshop underwent public-private partnership and later transformed into a modern enterprise, yet it has always adhered to traditional brewing methods. Today, Enquan stands as a representative of Jiangxi's food culture, carrying the taste memories of generations and embodying the culinary wisdom of the Gan-Po region.

Signature Products

Enquan Premium Soy Sauce

Crafted through traditional high-salt dilute-state fermentation, this soy sauce features a reddish-brown hue, rich aroma, and mellow umami flavor. Ideal for braising and cold dishes. (15-30 RMB per bottle)

Enquan Fermented Black Beans

Made from selected black soybeans through steaming, fermentation, and sun-drying, these beans are plump and full-bodied with a pronounced fermented aroma. Perfect for steaming fish or stir-frying meat. (10-20 RMB per pack)

Enquan Spicy Chili Sauce

Blending chili peppers and fermented black beans, this sauce is spicy yet not harsh, with a savory and appetizing taste. Great for tossing with noodles or as a dipping sauce. (12-25 RMB per bottle)

Cultural Note

Enquan's brewing technique follows ancient methods, insisting on natural fermentation through 'sun-drying and dew-exposure' (ri shai ye lu), and respecting the seasonal rhythm of 'spring koji, summer mash, autumn soy' (chun qu, xia jiang, qiu you). The craft emphasizes the selection of ingredients and the microbial environment, reflecting the essence of Jiangxi's traditional food culture. Enquan products are not just condiments but carriers of local culinary heritage, evoking nostalgia and cherished memories.
