Brand Story

Dingfeng traces its roots to Shanghai, with a history dating back to the late Qing Dynasty and early Republic of China. Originally a local sauce garden, it was famous for its traditional brewing methods and gradually evolved into a comprehensive food enterprise. The name 'Dingfeng' symbolizes prosperity and abundance, reflecting the founders' aspirations for business success. During the Republican era, Dingfeng products were already popular in Shanghai, becoming a staple on family dining tables. After the founding of New China, Dingfeng underwent public-private partnership, preserving traditional techniques while introducing modern management, thus expanding its product range. Its soy sauce, fermented using a high-salt dilute-state method, boasts a rich aroma and mellow taste, while its fermented bean curd is crafted through multiple processes for a delicate texture. Dingfeng is not just a company; it is a microcosm of Shanghai's food culture, carrying the taste memories of generations. Though its exact founding year remains unverified, its status in the Jiangnan food industry is undeniable, exemplifying the resilience and innovation of China's time-honored brands.

Cultural Note

Dingfeng's brewing techniques inherit the traditions of Jiangnan, emphasizing natural fermentation and the passage of time. The soy sauce undergoes months of sun exposure and dew, while the fermented bean curd requires careful selection of cultures and brine. This craft integrates microbiology with empirical wisdom, reflecting the Chinese culinary philosophy of balancing flavor and nourishment. The brand adheres to ancient methods while incorporating modern quality control to ensure consistent taste, making it a living testament to Shanghai-style seasoning culture.
