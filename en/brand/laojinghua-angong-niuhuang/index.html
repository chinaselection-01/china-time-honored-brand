Brand Story

Lao Jinghua, originating from Liaoning, is a time-honored brand in the field of traditional Chinese medicine and health. Although its exact founding year has not been officially verified, local historical records suggest its predecessor was a folk pharmacy dating back to the Qing Dynasty (1644-1912). During that era, epidemics frequently struck the Liaoshen region, and the pharmacy's owner, using ancestral secret formulas, prepared pills, powders, ointments, and pellets that proved highly effective, earning the trust of the local populace. In the Republican period (1912-1949), the pharmacy was renamed 'Lao Jinghua,' meaning 'retain the essence, discard the dross,' and gradually developed a unique pharmaceutical process. After the founding of the People's Republic of China, Lao Jinghua adapted to the changing times by preserving traditional processing methods while incorporating modern pharmaceutical technology, always adhering to the ancestral motto: 'authentic medicinal materials, meticulous workmanship.' Its cultural significance lies in being a microcosm of Liaoning's development in traditional Chinese medicine, carrying the simple beliefs of the people of the Guandong region regarding health and life. However, due to the loss of historical documents, the specific founding year and key figures' names remain to be further verified.

Cultural Note

Lao Jinghua adheres to the pharmaceutical principle: 'Even if the processing is complex, never reduce the effort; even if the ingredients are precious, never cut corners.' The brand emphasizes the authenticity of medicinal materials and traditional craftsmanship. Its pill production employs the pan-coating method, involving dozens of steps such as pellet initiation, shaping, and finishing, taking several days to ensure slow-release efficacy. Additionally, the brand integrates medicine with health preservation, advocating the concept of 'treating before illness,' reflecting the holistic and preventive views of traditional Chinese medicine.
