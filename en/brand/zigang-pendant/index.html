Brand Story

Zigang, originally named Lu Zigang, was an active jade carver during the Jiajing and Wanli reigns of the Ming Dynasty (1522-1620) in Suzhou. His exceptional skill earned him the title of the foremost 'Wuzhong unique craft'. Lu Zigang pioneered the practice of inscribing his name on jade pieces, using 'Zigang' or 'Zigang' as his mark, which revolutionized jade art. His works are characterized by meticulous material selection, ingenious design, bold lines, and elegant ornamentation. The 'Zigang plaque' (Zigang pai), which integrates poetry, calligraphy, painting, and seal carving into a jade tablet, became a model for later literati jade carvings. The Zigang craft has been passed down through generations, and despite dynastic changes, his name and style remain revered, making Zigang a significant cultural symbol of Suzhou jade carving. The Zigang brand carries the aesthetic and craftsmanship spirit of Ming Dynasty literati, an indispensable chapter in Chinese jade culture.

Cultural Note

Zigang's technique is noted for its 'empty, floating, and fine' qualities, emphasizing smooth lines and vivid forms. His unique mark-engraving process, often placed at the base or hidden spots, became a key authentication feature. Zigang's style influenced jade carving for centuries, and Suzhou jade carving gained fame through his legacy.
