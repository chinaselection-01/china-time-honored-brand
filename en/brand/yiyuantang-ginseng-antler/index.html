Brand Story

Yiyuantang, originating from Liaoning, derives its name from the principle of 'benefiting the origin and consolidating the foundation,' embodying the profound heritage of traditional Chinese medicine. Although its exact founding year lacks official verification, local chronicles and oral histories trace its roots to the mid-to-late Qing Dynasty (around 1800 AD), when Liaoning's thriving commerce spurred a growing demand for medicine. The founder, whose name awaits confirmation, established the brand with the mission of 'healing and saving lives,' sourcing authentic herbs and refining preparation techniques, gradually earning a reputation across the Liaoshen region. Through generations, Yiyuantang has adhered to 'selecting premium materials and employing meticulous craftsmanship,' integrating northern cold-region wellness wisdom into its formulas and preparations. Beyond a pharmacy, Yiyuantang serves as a cultural ambassador for traditional Chinese medicine, blending commercial ethics with medical practice and bearing witness to the historical evolution of healthcare in Liaoning.
