Brand Story

BOHUACAI, originating from Xiamen, Fujian, is a brand renowned for its craftsmanship. The name symbolizes 'gathering the essence of Chinese culture,' reflecting the profound cultural heritage of the Minnan region. Historical records show that Xiamen has been a vital port on the Maritime Silk Road since the Ming and Qing dynasties, with a thriving handicraft industry, particularly known for techniques like lacquer thread carving (qixian diao) and bead embroidery. Although the exact founding year of BOHUACAI is yet to be confirmed, its craft lineage can be traced back to folk workshops of the Qing Dynasty (1644-1912). The brand's key figures are inheritors of a local craft family, who have dedicated generations to mastering lacquer thread carving and bodiless lacquerware, seamlessly integrating traditional methods with contemporary design. Culturally, BOHUACAI is not merely a manufacturer of crafts but a guardian of Minnan cultural memory. Its works often draw inspiration from Buddhist themes and local folk customs, showcasing the unique charm of regional culture. The brand's story is passed down by word of mouth in Xiamen's old town, making it a symbol of local cultural identity.

Cultural Note

BOHUACAI's craftsmanship centers on lacquer thread carving, a complex process involving dozens of steps such as base preparation, thread twisting, coiling, and lacquering, which can take weeks to months to complete. Its cultural value lies in preserving Minnan folk beliefs and aesthetics, with works often used in temple decorations and home furnishings, embodying the artisan spirit of 'meticulous workmanship and conveying philosophy through objects.'
