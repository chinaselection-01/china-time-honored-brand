Brand Story

Yingzhou, historically known as Ruyin, is located in the northwest of Anhui Province, a region with a long and splendid cultural history. The jewelry brand 'Yingzhou' takes its name from this ancient land, carrying a legacy of millennia. Historical records show that Yingzhou was a thriving commercial hub since the Han Dynasty, and during the Tang and Song dynasties, its jewelry and jade trade flourished thanks to the Grand Canal. The brand was founded in modern times, with the exact year yet to be officially confirmed. Its founder, deeply influenced by Yingzhou's culture, aspired to merge traditional jewelry craftsmanship with local heritage. The techniques draw from both ancient imperial and folk arts, particularly excelling in filigree inlay (huasi xiangqian) and jade carving. The brand emphasizes not only the beauty of its jewelry but also its cultural significance; each piece carries auspicious meanings such as 'blessings and longevity' or 'dragon and phoenix bringing prosperity,' making it beloved by locals. The rise of Yingzhou jewelry is not merely a continuation of traditional craftsmanship but also a celebration of regional culture, serving as a bridge between past and present, and showcasing the cultural confidence and artisan spirit of a time-honored Chinese brand.
