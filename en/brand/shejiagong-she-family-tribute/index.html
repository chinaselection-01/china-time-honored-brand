Brand Story

Shejia Gong, originating from Anhui, bears a name steeped in regional culture. According to existing historical records, the exact founding year of the brand has not been officially verified, but its roots can be traced back to the Ming and Qing dynasties, a period marked by the prosperity of Huizhou merchants. Legend has it that the She family operated their business for generations, upholding integrity and combining local specialties with exquisite craftsmanship, gradually forming a unique brand style. During the Qing Dynasty, Shejia Gong products were presented as local tributes to the imperial court, hence the name 'Shejia Gong' (literally 'Tribute of the She Family'). The products are primarily handmade, with a strong emphasis on the selection of raw materials and attention to craft details, reflecting the Huizhou people's spirit of meticulous craftsmanship. Although key figures and specific anecdotes are yet to be verified, the cultural significance of Shejia Gong lies in its witness to the inheritance of commerce and handicraft in the Anhui region, serving as a microcosm of local history. Today, Shejia Gong continues to uphold tradition and is committed to carrying forward this cultural heritage.
