Brand Story

Located at the intersection of Huaihai Middle Road and Sinan Road, Changchun Food Store traces its origins to the public-private partnership era of the 1950s. In 1958, under the umbrella of the Shanghai Sugar, Tobacco, and Wine Company, the store officially opened, its name symbolizing 'everlasting spring' and the owners' wish for thriving business and loyal customers. During the planned economy period, it became a vital source for candies, pastries, cigarettes, and wine, earning a reputation as a 'trustworthy store' through strict sourcing and excellent service. After the reform and opening-up, it pioneered the introduction of regional specialties and created its own 'Changchun' product line, including mooncakes and pastries, establishing itself as a landmark on Huaihai Road. Over decades of market changes, it has upheld the principle of 'quality first, customers foremost,' with its bulk candies and freshly baked pork mooncakes remaining classic flavors in Shanghai's collective memory. In the 2010s, the store underwent renovation, blending tradition with modernity while continuing to offer premium food and service.

Cultural Note

Changchun Food Store epitomizes the commercial culture of Huaihai Road, reflecting the meticulousness and integrity of Shanghai-style business. Its practice of 'front store, back workshop' (with some products made fresh on-site) emphasizes freshness and customer experience, a tradition increasingly rare in today's fast-paced commerce. The store's preservation of old-fashioned sales methods, such as bulk weighing and counter service, serves as a vessel of urban memory, mirroring Shanghainese nostalgia for traditional lifestyles.
