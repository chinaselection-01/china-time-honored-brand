Brand Story

Qiao, a traditional Sichuan food brand, takes its name from bridges, which historically linked towns and villages in the region. While the exact founding year remains unverified, local records suggest its origins trace back to the mid-to-late Qing Dynasty (around 1800 AD), when commerce flourished and bridges became vital arteries of trade. The brand began by crafting traditional foods, blending unique Sichuan seasoning techniques to develop a distinctive style. Although details of the founder are yet to be confirmed, it is said that early artisans skillfully used local ingredients and folk recipes to create flavorful delicacies beloved by the people. Culturally, Qiao represents not only physical connections but also the transmission and exchange of culinary traditions, embodying Sichuan's dedication to gastronomy and wisdom.

Cultural Note

Qiao's craftsmanship preserves traditional Sichuan food processing methods, emphasizing ingredient selection and flavor balance, reflecting the essence of Sichuan cuisine: 'one dish, one style; a hundred dishes, a hundred flavors.' Some production steps, such as pressing dried tofu and roasting beef jerky, are still done by hand, making these techniques integral to local food culture.
