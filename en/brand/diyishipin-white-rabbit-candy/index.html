Brand Story

The history of Diyi Food dates back to the mid-20th century, originating from a retail store under the Shanghai Sugar, Tobacco, and Wine Company. In 1954, to meet the demand for quality food, the company opened its first large food store on East Nanjing Road, becoming the largest food retail venue in Shanghai at the time. Over the decades, Diyi Food underwent multiple expansions and renovations, always adhering to the philosophy of 'Quality First, Service Supreme.' It gathered renowned foods from across the country, making it the preferred destination for Shanghai residents to purchase New Year goods and gifts. After the reform and opening-up, Diyi Food pioneered self-service shopping, transforming traditional retail into modern commerce. In the 21st century, leveraging its brand strength, it expanded online and continuously innovated its product lines, carrying forward the legacy of this time-honored brand. Diyi Food is not just a store; it is a microcosm of Shanghai's commercial culture, witnessing the evolution of urban consumption and carrying the taste memories of generations.
