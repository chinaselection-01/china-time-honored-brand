Brand Story

Cailinji was established in the 1930s (around 1930, during the Republic of China era) by Cai Mingwei and his wife in Hankou's Changdi Street. Cai improved traditional noodles by adding sesame paste, sesame oil, and other seasonings, creating the distinctive 'hot dry noodles' that quickly became popular in Wuhan. The name combines the surname 'Cai' and the character 'Lin' (referring to the woods near the shop), symbolizing prosperity. Known for their smooth, chewy texture and rich sesame flavor, Cailinji's noodles became a breakfast staple for Wuhan residents, witnessing the city's modern commercial and culinary development. Despite wars and changes, Cailinji has upheld its traditional techniques, becoming an important symbol of Wuhan's urban memory.

Hot Dry Noodles

Three Delicacies Bean Skin

Egg Wine

Cultural Note

The craft of making Cailinji hot dry noodles involves precise steps like 'drying the noodles' (dan mian) and 'mixing the sauce' (ban jiang), with sesame paste blended with sesame oil. This reflects Wuhan people's culinary wisdom of combining convenience and flavor. As a representative of Wuhan's breakfast culture, it has been listed in Hubei Province's intangible cultural heritage, serving as a valuable sample for studying modern Chinese urban dietary changes.
