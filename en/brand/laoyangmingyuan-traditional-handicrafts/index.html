Brand Story

Lao Yang Mingyuan, originating from Hunan, carries a name rich in meaning, symbolizing the inheritance and memory of local craftsmanship. Although the exact founding year has yet to be officially confirmed, local historical records and oral traditions suggest that its techniques can be traced back to the Ming and Qing dynasties (1368-1912), when Hunan's handicraft industry flourished. It was during this period that the predecessor of Lao Yang Mingyuan began to take shape. The brand is celebrated for its exquisite craftsmanship and honest business practices. Through the dedication and innovation of successive generations, it gradually earned a stellar reputation in the region. Key figures include members of the founding family, who not only passed down the skills but also infused cultural connotations into their products, making them an integral part of Huxiang culture. Lao Yang Mingyuan is more than just a brand; it is a living fossil of local history and folk customs. Its products bear witness to the changes in everyday life and embody people's respect and nostalgia for traditional craftsmanship. Although specific dates remain to be verified, its cultural significance is profound, serving as an important reference for studying the economic and handicraft development of Hunan.
