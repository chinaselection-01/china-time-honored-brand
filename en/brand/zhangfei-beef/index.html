Brand Story

The Zhang Fei brand originates from Sichuan, with its exact founding year yet to be determined. Rooted in the culture of the Three Kingdoms, particularly the legendary image of Zhang Fei in Shu, the brand is dedicated to preserving Sichuan's culinary traditions. Legend has it that Zhang Fei was not only a brave warrior but also a skilled cook, and his braising techniques were passed down among the people. Inspired by this tale, the brand's founders integrated modern food processing to create delicacies with cultural significance. Named directly after the historical figure, the brand aims to promote the spirit of loyalty and righteousness alongside Bashu's food culture. Its main products include braised meats and condiments, adhering to traditional recipes and using high-quality local ingredients, which have won the hearts of consumers. Blending history with commerce, the brand has become a representative of Sichuan-style cuisine.

Cultural Note

The brand is deeply rooted in Three Kingdoms culture, with Zhang Fei's bold and loyal character resonating with the bold and spicy flavors of Sichuan cuisine. The production techniques follow traditional braising methods, emphasizing the balance of spices and precise heat control, reflecting the essence of Sichuan cuisine: 'Each dish has its own style, and a hundred dishes have a hundred flavors.' Additionally, the packaging design incorporates elements from the Three Kingdoms era, enhancing cultural recognition.
