Brand Story

Originating in Gansu, the Feitian brand draws its name and inspiration from the flying Apsaras depicted in the murals of the Mogao Caves in Dunhuang. Gansu has long been a vital hub on the ancient Silk Road, and Dunhuang's art represents a remarkable fusion of Eastern and Western cultures. The brand emerged against the backdrop of a revival in Gansu's arts and crafts during the late 20th century, though its precise founding year has yet to be officially confirmed. Feitian symbolizes freedom, grace, and beauty, and its crafts blend traditional techniques with modern design to capture the unique allure of Dunhuang art. Key figures include local master artisans who strive to transform the ethereal Apsaras from wall paintings into tangible works of art. Culturally, Feitian is not only a tribute to Dunhuang's artistic heritage but also a living continuation of China's rich cultural traditions.

Cultural Note

The brand employs traditional techniques such as cloisonné (filigree inlay), embroidery, and ceramics, all inspired by Dunhuang murals. Emphasizing flowing lines and rich color gradations, the crafts reflect a fusion of Chinese and Western influences. Each piece undergoes meticulous multi-step processes, honoring ancient methods while incorporating modern aesthetics, showcasing the distinctive character of Gansu.
