Brand Story

Founded in early 20th-century Shanghai, Yan Yun Lou's exact founding year remains unverified. Its name, derived from the 'Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun,' signifies authentic Beijing style. As one of the earliest Beijing cuisine restaurants in Shanghai, it gained fame for roast duck and braised dishes, merging northern culinary traditions with Shanghai's elegance. Although the founder's identity is unrecorded, the restaurant witnessed the fusion of diverse food cultures in modern Shanghai. Through decades of change, Yan Yun Lou has upheld traditional techniques, serving as a key inheritor of Beijing cuisine in Shanghai and a living part of the city's gastronomic history.

Cultural Note

Yan Yun Lou's culinary skills follow the Beijing-Shandong school, emphasizing precise heat control and slow-simmered broths to preserve natural flavors. Its roast duck uses traditional hanging-oven technique, while braised dishes showcase the essence of 'braising' mastery. These techniques evolved with Shanghai's commercial prosperity, blending northern and southern tastes, making Yan Yun Lou a representative of Shanghai-style Beijing cuisine and a valuable living resource for studying modern Shanghai's food culture.
