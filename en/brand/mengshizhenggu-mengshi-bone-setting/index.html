Brand Story

Meng's Bone Setting originated in the Jilin region, with a history dating back to the mid-Qing Dynasty. According to legend, the Meng ancestors combined northern folk bone-setting experience with their medical practice, gradually developing a unique set of techniques. By the late Qing and early Republic period, Meng Xianzhang (c. 1850-1920), a descendant, opened a clinic in Jilin City, specializing in fractures, dislocations, and soft tissue injuries. Known for his gentle yet effective methods, he gained local fame. The practice emphasizes 'hand-touch and heart-understanding, technique flowing from the hands,' integrating TCM qi-blood theory with local reduction. It forms a three-in-one system of 'bone-setting, tendon-regulating, and herbal medication.' Their secret plasters and oral formulas use authentic Changbai Mountain herbs, prepared with meticulous care and passed down through generations. Meng's Bone Setting is not just a medical skill but also a carrier of Northeast folk culture and family memory, embodying the unique wisdom of TCM orthopedics.
