Brand Story

Huang Tianyuan was founded during the Daoguang reign of the Qing Dynasty (1821-1850) by a Mr. Huang from Suzhou. It began as a humble street vendor selling rice cakes and dumplings. Thanks to its meticulous selection of ingredients and exquisite craftsmanship, it gradually gained a reputation throughout the city. During the Tongzhi reign (1862-1875), Huang Tianyuan opened a permanent shop on Guanqian Street, officially establishing its brand. The founder adhered to the business philosophy of 'sincerity to customers, quality for success,' incorporating seasonal Jiangnan ingredients into their pastries, creating signature items like osmanthus cake and mint cake. Huang Tianyuan's products not only won the hearts of locals but also became an indispensable part of Suzhou's folk festivals, such as New Year rice cakes, Dragon Boat Festival zongzi, and Mid-Autumn mooncakes. Through generations of inheritance, Huang Tianyuan has preserved traditional techniques while adapting to modern times, becoming an iconic brand of Suzhou's culinary culture.

Cultural Note

Huang Tianyuan's pastry-making techniques follow the traditions of Suzhou-style pastries, emphasizing seasonal variations, meticulous ingredient selection, and fine craftsmanship. Its products are closely tied to Suzhou's folk customs, such as eating rice cakes during the Spring Festival to symbolize 'rising year after year,' and enjoying zongzi during the Dragon Boat Festival to commemorate Qu Yuan. Huang Tianyuan insists on handmade production and preserves ancient methods, serving as an important carrier of Jiangnan culinary culture.
