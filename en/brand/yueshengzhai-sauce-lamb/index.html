Brand Story

Yueshengzhai was founded around 1775 during the Qianlong reign of the Qing Dynasty by the Hui Muslim Ma family near Qianmen, Beijing. The founder, Ma Qingrui, learned secret braising techniques while assisting in the imperial kitchen. He later set up his own workshop, gaining fame for his meticulous ingredient selection, precise cooking, and rich flavors. Historical records like 'Dumen Jilue' document its prosperity, and even Empress Dowager Cixi is said to have tasted and praised its products. Through generations, Yueshengzhai has upheld traditional methods, blending halal dietary practices with Beijing's regional character. Its craftsmanship is now recognized as a National Intangible Cultural Heritage, symbolizing the enduring legacy of China's time-honored brands.

Cultural Note

Yueshengzhai's braising technique emphasizes 'precise ingredients, accurate seasonings, and steady heat,' using a traditional aged marinade that is continuously replenished to develop its unique flavor. The process integrates Islamic dietary norms with Beijing's folk cooking wisdom, making it a living testament to halal culinary culture. Beyond food, this brand is a cherished part of Beijing's urban memory, evoking nostalgic tastes across generations.
