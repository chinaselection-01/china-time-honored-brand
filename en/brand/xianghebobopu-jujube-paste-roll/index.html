Brand Story

Xianghe Bobo Pu, originating from Tianjin, takes its name from 'bobo,' a Manchu word for pastry, echoing the culinary legacy of the Qing imperial court. Its techniques are said to trace back to the imperial kitchens of the late Qing dynasty, later passed down through generations of artisans. In 2015, the brand was formally established with a mission to revive traditional bobo pastries. Combining the best of northern and southern pastry traditions, it insists on handmade craftsmanship and carefully selected ingredients. The founder, influenced by family heritage, skillfully blends ancient methods with modern flavors, giving new life to time-honored tastes. Beyond selling pastries, Xianghe Bobo Pu is dedicated to spreading Chinese food etiquette and culture. Its products, such as jujube paste rolls and imperial concubine cakes, are steeped in historical anecdotes, serving as a delicious bridge between past and present. In today's fast-paced urban life, Xianghe upholds the warmth of traditional craftsmanship, allowing customers to savor the cultural depth of century-old pastries.

Cultural Note

Xianghe Bobo Pu adheres to traditional handmade techniques, including layering dough, filling, and baking, all following ancient methods. Its pastries blend Manchu and Han culinary customs, reflecting the cultural fusion of the Beijing-Tianjin region. The brand insists on using natural ingredients, avoiding additives, and upholds the health philosophy of 'eating seasonally.' By reviving traditional bobo pastries, Xianghe brings nearly lost craftsmanship back to life, becoming a tasteful carrier of Tianjin's urban memory.
