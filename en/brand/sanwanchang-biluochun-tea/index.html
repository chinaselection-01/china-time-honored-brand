Brand Story

Founded in 1855 during the Qing Dynasty's Xianfeng era, San Wan Chang was established by a tea merchant surnamed Shen inside Changmen Gate in Suzhou. The name, meaning 'Three Yangs Bring Prosperity, Ten Thousand Generations of Flourishing,' reflected auspicious hopes. Shen's strict quality control and honest business practices quickly earned local trust. Under his son Shen Tingzhen, during the Guangxu period (1875-1908), the brand expanded with a branch on Guanqian Street and direct sourcing from Anhui and Zhejiang tea regions. During the Republic era, it became a favored gathering spot for literati, blending with Suzhou's Pingtan storytelling culture, giving rise to the saying, 'Drink tea at San Wan Chang, listen to stories at Guangyu Society.' After 1956, the brand faded but its original site remained. In 2000, the Suzhou Tea Company revived the brand, preserving traditional techniques and restoring its glory.

Signature Products

Biluochun Green Tea

Produced from Dongting Mountain, this tea features slender, spiral-shaped leaves with a rich floral and fruity aroma. It is a representative of Suzhou green teas. Price: 200-800 RMB per 500g.

Jasmine Tea

Made from baked green tea scented with jasmine flowers, it offers a fresh, lingering floral fragrance, a bright yellow infusion, and a mellow taste. Price: 100-300 RMB per 500g.

Kung Fu Black Tea

Selected from Jiangnan bush varieties, this tea is fermented to a balanced degree, yielding a sweet, honeyed flavor with a hint of malt. Price: 150-500 RMB per 500g.

Cultural Note

San Wan Chang adheres to traditional tea-making techniques: Biluochun is hand-fried with the method 'hands never leave the tea, tea never leaves the wok,' while jasmine tea undergoes a 'seven-scenting, one-drying' process. Its business philosophy, 'genuine goods at fair prices, honest with all customers,' is woven into Suzhou's tea culture, integrating with Pingtan and classical gardens, making it a local cultural icon.
