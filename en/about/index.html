About China Time-Honored Brands

china-time-honored-brand.com is positioned as “China’s #1 time-honored brand portal” — the official information hub, curated shop and digital services for every legacy brand. This is an MVP demo showcasing the product shape and business logic.

A Three-Layer Business Model

L1 Traffic

An SEO-driven brand portal that accumulates category-wide traffic and builds a content moat hard to replicate.

L2 Monetization

Curated shop + gifting procurement matchmaking, commission on GMV, launched with zero inventory.

L3 Value-added

Enterprise SaaS, IP licensing matchmaking and industry data — 70%+ gross margin.

Why time-honored brands join

  • · Brand pages: built-in SEO traffic, long-term accumulation of reputation and search authority.
  • · Digital marketing: WeChat / Douyin / RED content operations to reach younger audiences.
